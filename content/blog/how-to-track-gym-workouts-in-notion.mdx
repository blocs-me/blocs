## How to Set Up a Workout Log in Notion

Start with a simple database. This is the backbone of your gym tracker and requires nothing beyond a free Notion account.

### Step 1: Create a Workout Log Database

In a new Notion page, type `/table` and create an inline database. Add these properties:

- **Name** (default): The workout name or focus (e.g., "Push Day", "Leg Day")
- **Date** (Date property): When you trained
- **Exercises** (Text or Multi-select): Movements covered
- **Duration** (Number): Minutes in the gym
- **Notes** (Text): How it felt, PRs, adjustments
- **Rating** (Select): 1-5 scale for session quality

Each row becomes one workout session. Over time, filtering by week or month gives you a clear picture of training frequency and volume.

### Step 2: Add Views for Better Visibility

Notion lets you create multiple views of the same database. Add a **Calendar view** to see which days you trained at a glance, and a **Gallery view** grouped by muscle group or workout type. This takes about two minutes and makes the database considerably more useful than a flat list.

### Step 3: Create a Template for Each Session

Use Notion's database templates to pre-fill your exercise list for recurring workouts. Create a "Push Day" template that already has bench press, overhead press, and tricep work listed. When you log a session, you open the template and fill in the numbers rather than starting from scratch.

## FAQs

### Can I track workouts in Notion for free?

Yes. Notion's free tier supports databases, templates, and multiple views. The Blocs Habit Tracker and Pomodoro Timer are also free to embed. The only features behind a paywall are Blocs Pro extras like the Countdown Timer and Progress Bar.

### Do I need to install anything to use Blocs widgets in Notion?

No installation required. Use Notion's `/embed` block and paste the widget URL. The widget loads inline as an iframe. No browser extension, no third-party integration.

### Can I log individual exercises, sets, and reps in Notion?

Yes. A Notion database with text, number, and select properties can capture all of this. For granular tracking (each set as a row), use a sub-database linked to each workout entry. For simpler logging, a text block per exercise in the page body works fine.

### Is there a Notion template for gym tracking?

Notion's template gallery includes several fitness trackers. You can also build one from scratch in under 10 minutes using the structure above. Adding Blocs widgets on top of any template gives you the interactive elements Notion can't provide natively.

### Can I use my Notion gym tracker on mobile?

Notion's mobile app supports databases and most block types. Blocs widgets embedded via iframe may not render in the Notion mobile app due to platform restrictions, but they work fully in any mobile browser.

### Does Blocs sync data across devices?

Cloud sync is a Blocs Pro feature. Free widgets reset if you clear browser data. Pro users get persistent data tied to their account, accessible from any device.
