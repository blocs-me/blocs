## What's the best way to track subscriptions in Notion?

The best way is a single database (table view) with one row per subscription, tracking name, monthly or annual cost, billing date, and category. Add a formula property that calculates days until renewal so you can sort or filter for anything due soon, turning a static list into an active budgeting tool.

## How do I build a subscription tracker database in Notion?

Create a new database and add these properties, then populate one row per service you pay for:

### 1. Set up the core properties

- **Name** (Title) - e.g., Netflix, Spotify, Adobe Creative Cloud
- **Cost** (Number, formatted as currency)
- **Billing Cycle** (Select: Monthly, Yearly, Weekly)
- **Next Renewal** (Date)
- **Category** (Select: Streaming, Software, Fitness, etc.)
- **Status** (Select: Active, Cancelled, Trial)

### 2. Add a "days left" formula

Use a Formula property like `dateBetween(prop("Next Renewal"), now(), "days")` to show how many days remain. Sort the table by this column ascending so the most urgent renewals sit at the top.

### 3. Create views for spending insight

Add a Board view grouped by Category to see where money goes, and a filtered view that only shows subscriptions renewing in the next 7 days, useful for catching anything you meant to cancel.

## FAQs

### Do I need a Notion template to track subscriptions?

No. A basic database with Name, Cost, Billing Cycle, and Next Renewal properties covers most people's needs and takes a few minutes to set up from scratch, no template download required.

### Can Notion automatically remind me before a subscription renews?

Notion doesn't send push notifications for date properties by default, though you can use Notion's built-in reminders on a date field to get an in-app or email alert a day before renewal.

### Is tracking subscriptions in Notion free?

Yes, building the database itself is free on any Notion plan. Only optional visual add-ons, like Blocs' Countdown Timer widget, require a separate one-time payment.

### How do I see my total monthly subscription cost?

Add a formula or use the "Sum" calculation at the bottom of your Cost column, filtered to Monthly billing cycle, to get a running total of what you pay each month.

### Can I track both monthly and annual subscriptions in one table?

Yes. Use the Billing Cycle select property to separate them, then create a formula that normalizes annual costs to a monthly equivalent (annual cost divided by 12) for accurate side-by-side comparison.
