## What's the best way to track grades in Notion?

The best way to track grades in Notion is a database (table or board view) with one row per assignment, a "Weight" number property, a "Score" number property, and a formula property that multiplies the two to calculate your weighted average per course. This avoids manual math and updates automatically as you log new grades.

### Step 1: Create the grades database

Add a new full-page database and create these properties:

- **Course** (select or relation to a Classes database)
- **Assignment** (title)
- **Category** (select: Homework, Quiz, Exam, Project)
- **Weight (%)** (number)
- **Score (%)** (number)

### Step 2: Add the weighted average formula

Create a formula property that multiplies Weight by Score for each row, then group by Course and use a Sum rollup divided by total weight to get your running course average. If you prefer a simpler version, a basic formula like `prop("Score") * (prop("Weight") / 100)` works for per-assignment contribution, and you can total it at the group level.

### Step 3: Convert average to a letter grade (optional)

Use an `if()` formula chain to convert your numeric average into a letter grade, e.g. 90+ = A, 80-89 = B, and so on. This is a common pattern in Notion's own [formula documentation](https://www.notion.com/help/formulas) for conditional logic.

## FAQs

### Can Notion automatically calculate my GPA?

Yes, using a formula property that converts letter or percentage grades to a GPA scale (e.g., A=4.0, B=3.0) and averages them across courses, weighted by credit hours if needed. This requires manual formula setup but runs automatically afterward.

### Is there a free Notion grade tracker template?

Notion's own template gallery and many community creators offer free grade tracker templates with pre-built formulas. You can also build your own in under 15 minutes using the steps above if you want full control over categories and weighting.
