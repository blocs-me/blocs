## What is 75 Hard?

75 Hard is a 75-day mental toughness challenge created by entrepreneur Andy Frisella that requires five non-negotiable daily tasks: two 45-minute workouts (one outdoors), following a diet with zero alcohol or cheat meals, drinking a gallon of water, reading 10 pages of a non-fiction book, and taking a daily progress photo. Missing any single task on any day means starting over from Day 1.

## What should my 75 Hard daily checklist include?

A complete 75 Hard checklist has exactly 5 items repeated daily for 75 days straight: two 45-minute workouts (one must be outdoors regardless of weather), a strict diet with no alcohol and no cheat meals, one gallon of water, 10 pages of a non-fiction or self-improvement book, and one progress photo.

- Workout 1 (45 min, indoor or outdoor)
- Workout 2 (45 min, must be outdoors)
- Diet adherence (no alcohol, no cheat meals)
- 1 gallon of water
- 10 pages of reading (non-fiction/self-help)
