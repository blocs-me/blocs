## How to Embed a Professional Notion Widget

1. Go to the widget page on [blocs.me](https://blocs.me) and copy the embed URL (e.g., `https://blocs.me/pomodoro`).
2. Open the Notion page where you want the widget.
3. Type `/embed` and select the Embed block.
4. Paste the widget URL and click Embed Link.
5. Resize the block to fit your layout.

No code, no integrations, no Zapier required. The widget loads directly inside Notion and persists across sessions when you're signed in to Pro.
