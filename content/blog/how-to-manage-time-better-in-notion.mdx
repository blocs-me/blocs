## Why Does Notion Lack Built-In Time Management Tools?

Notion is a powerful note-taking and project management tool, but it is deliberately designed as a document and database platform — not a time-tracking or scheduling app. As of mid-2026, it has no native timer, no habit streak counter, and no visual countdown to a deadline. Most users end up bouncing between Notion and a separate timer app, which breaks focus and defeats the purpose of having a unified workspace.

The practical fix is embeds. Notion supports iframes natively through its **/embed** block, which means any web-based widget with a shareable URL can live inside your pages. This is exactly what makes tools like [Blocs](https://blocs.me) so useful: the widgets load inside Notion itself, not in a separate browser tab.

## How Do I Add a Pomodoro Timer to Notion?

To add a Pomodoro timer to Notion, type `/embed` in any Notion page, paste `https://blocs.me/pomodoro` into the URL field, and press Enter. The timer loads inline — start a 25-minute focus session, take a 5-minute break, and repeat. No sign-up or app install needed. This is the fastest way to bring structured time blocking into your Notion workspace.

The Pomodoro Technique, originally developed by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s, breaks work into 25-minute focused intervals separated by short breaks. Decades of productivity research support interval-based work as a way to reduce mental fatigue and maintain concentration. Having the timer embedded directly on your task list page means the timer and your work context are always visible together.

## How Do I Set Up Blocs Widgets in Notion?

1. Open any Notion page where you want the widget.
2. Type `/embed` and select the "Embed" block option.
3. Paste the widget URL (e.g., `https://blocs.me/pomodoro`) and click "Embed link".
4. Resize the embed block by dragging the edges to fit your layout.
5. For Pro widgets, sign in at [blocs.me/sign-in](https://blocs.me/sign-in) first so your settings sync across devices.

The free widgets (Pomodoro, Habit Tracker, Water Tracker) work immediately — no account needed.
