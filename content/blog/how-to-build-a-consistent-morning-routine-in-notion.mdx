## How Do You Set Up a Morning Routine Page in Notion?

Create a new Notion page titled "Morning Routine", then divide it into three sections using H3 headings: "Morning Checklist", "Focus Block", and "Daily Wellness". This structure keeps the page scannable and ensures each part of your routine has a dedicated space. Use a Notion template button to reset checkboxes each day without duplicating pages.

### Step 1 — Build Your Morning Checklist

Add a checklist block at the top of the page. Keep it to 5-8 items — research on [habit stacking from James Clear](https://jamesclear.com/atomic-habits) shows smaller, concrete actions are more likely to be completed consistently. Example items:

- Drink a glass of water
- 10-minute stretch or movement
- Review today's top 3 priorities
- Focused deep work block (25 min)
- Read for 15 minutes

Use a Notion "Template Button" (type `/button`) to duplicate the checklist fresh each morning with one click.

## What Makes a Morning Routine Actually Stick?

Consistency comes from reducing the effort required to start. The biggest reasons people abandon morning routines are: the setup is too complex, tracking happens in a different app, and there's no feedback loop. A Notion page with embedded widgets solves all three — your checklist, timer, habits, and hydration are one tap away, and Blocs provides the streak data that makes progress visible.

- **Keep it short:** A 45-60 minute routine is more sustainable than a 3-hour one. Protect your morning from scope creep.
- **Review weekly:** Check your habit tracker analytics every Sunday. Drop anything with fewer than 4 completions per week.
- **Stack habits:** Pair each checklist item with an existing trigger (coffee = open Notion, sitting down = start timer).
- **Don't break the chain:** A visible streak on your habit tracker is a low-key but powerful motivator — the [Seinfeld Strategy](https://jamesclear.com/dont-break-the-chain) applied to your Notion workspace.
