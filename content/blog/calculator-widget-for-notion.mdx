## What is a Calculator Widget for Notion?

A calculator widget for Notion is an embeddable tool that lets you perform arithmetic or goal-based calculations without leaving your Notion page. Because Notion supports [iframe embeds](https://www.notion.so/help/embed-and-connect-other-apps), any publicly accessible calculator URL can be dropped in as a live, interactive block — no extensions, no copy-paste math errors.

The use cases range from quick unit conversions on a recipe page to BMI calculators on a fitness dashboard to currency converters on a travel planner. The challenge is finding a calculator that is both iframe-friendly (many are blocked by `X-Frame-Options` headers) and visually clean inside Notion's minimal UI.

## How to Embed a Calculator in Notion

Embedding any widget in Notion takes under a minute. The steps below work for calculators, timers, and any other iframe-compatible tool.

1. Open the Notion page where you want the calculator.
2. Type `/embed` and press Enter to insert an embed block.
3. Paste the calculator URL into the field and click **Embed link**.
4. Resize the block by dragging the bottom edge to fit the calculator's layout.

If the embed shows a blank white box, the site blocks iframes. Try a different calculator source (see the options below) or use Notion formulas instead.

### Which Calculator URLs Work as Notion Embeds?

Not every calculator site allows iframe embedding. As of September 2026, the following types of sources are reliably embeddable:

- **Desmos** ([desmos.com/scientific](https://www.desmos.com/scientific)) — scientific calculator, fully iframe-friendly.
- **GeoGebra** ([geogebra.org/calculator](https://www.geogebra.org/calculator)) — graphing and scientific calculator.
- **Calculator.net widgets** — specialty calculators (BMI, mortgage, unit conversion) with embeddable widget codes.
- **Custom Notion embeds** via tools like Apption or Widgetbox, which offer pre-tested iframe URLs.

Google's standard calculator (`google.com/search?q=calculator`) does *not* embed — it blocks iframes by design.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can you embed a calculator in Notion?

Yes. Use the `/embed` block and paste a URL from an iframe-friendly calculator like Desmos or GeoGebra. Some popular calculator sites block iframes, so not every URL will work. Desmos Scientific ([desmos.com/scientific](https://www.desmos.com/scientific)) is the most reliable free option as of 2026.

### Does Notion have a built-in calculator?

Notion does not have a standalone calculator block. However, its **formula property** in databases performs arithmetic on row data, and its **AI assistant** can answer math questions inline. For a visual calculator widget, an iframe embed is the standard workaround.

### What is the best free calculator widget for Notion?

For general arithmetic, embed [Desmos Scientific](https://www.desmos.com/scientific) via the `/embed` block. For goal-based tracking math (time, water intake, habits), the free widgets from [Blocs](https://blocs.me) are purpose-built for Notion and require no sign-up.

### Why isn't my calculator embed working in Notion?

Most blank-embed issues are caused by the calculator site blocking iframes using an `X-Frame-Options: SAMEORIGIN` or `Content-Security-Policy` header. Google Calculator, for example, cannot be embedded anywhere. Switch to a source like Desmos or a widget service that explicitly supports embedding.
