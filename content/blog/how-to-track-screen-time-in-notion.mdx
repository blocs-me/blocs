## How do I track screen time in Notion?

Check your phone's built-in screen time total (Settings > Screen Time on iOS, or Settings > Digital Wellbeing on Android), then log that number into Notion once a day, either in a database row or by checking off a "screen time goal met" habit in an embedded widget. There's no native sync between your phone's OS and Notion, so the entry step is manual either way.

Most people pick one of three setups depending on how much detail they want:

- **Manual database:** A table with Date, Total Hours, Top App, and Notes properties. Best if you want to analyze trends later.
- **Daily habit check-in:** A single yes/no habit like "Under 2 hours today?" tracked in a widget. Best for quick daily accountability.
- **API automation:** Pulling exported screen time data into Notion via the [Notion API](https://developers.notion.com) and a no-code automation tool. Best for people comfortable setting up integrations.

## How do I build a manual screen time database?

Create a new Notion database with four properties: Date, Hours, Top App, and Notes. Add one row per day, type in the total from your phone's screen time report, and use a bar chart view (via a Notion chart embed or a simple linked gallery) to spot patterns over a week or month.

1. Create a new full-page database and name it "Screen Time Log."
2. Add properties: Date (date), Hours (number), Top App (text), Notes (text).
3. Each evening, open your phone's Screen Time or Digital Wellbeing report and copy the total into a new row.
4. Switch to a timeline or board view grouped by week to see trends.

This method gives you the most detail, but it's slower than a one-tap habit check, and it's easy to abandon after a week or two if the manual entry feels like a chore. ## Can I automate screen time data into Notion?

You can automate screen time logging by exporting data from apps like RescueTime or Toggl Track and routing it into a Notion database through the [Notion API](https://developers.notion.com) and a no-code tool such as Zapier or Make. This removes manual typing but requires setting up an integration and an API key.

At a high level: connect your screen time source to Zapier or Make, map the daily total to a Notion database property, and schedule the automation to run once a day. This is the most "hands-off" option, but it adds setup time and a dependency on a third-party tracking app, which is why most casual users stick with manual entry or a habit widget instead.

## FAQs

### Does Notion have a built-in screen time tracker?

No. Notion has no native screen time feature or OS-level integration. You either build a manual database, embed a habit-tracking widget, or connect a third-party app through the Notion API.

### Can I pull iPhone Screen Time into Notion automatically?

Not directly. Apple doesn't expose a public Screen Time API, so there's no official automatic sync. Most people copy the daily total from Settings > Screen Time by hand, or use a workaround through a separate tracking app that does offer an export.

### What's the fastest way to log screen time daily?
