## What should a good Notion habit tracker include?

A good Notion habit tracker should include daily check-off boxes, visible streak counts, and some form of progress history (weekly or monthly). These three elements map directly to the behavioral psychology behind habit formation: visible streaks create a "don't break the chain" incentive, while historical data lets you spot which habits you're actually sticking to.

- **Daily check-offs:** One tap or click per habit, per day. Friction kills habit tracking faster than anything else.
- **Streak counts:** Seeing "12 days in a row" is a stronger motivator than a plain checkbox grid, according to behavior-change research cited by [James Clear](https://jamesclear.com/new-habit), author of Atomic Habits.
- **History/analytics:** A weekly or monthly view shows patterns, like which habits consistently slip on weekends, that a single day's checklist can't reveal.
- **Unlimited habit slots:** Many free tools cap you at 3-5 habits. If you're tracking more than that, check the limit before committing to a tool.

## FAQs

### Can I track habits in Notion for free?

Yes. Both native Notion databases and Blocs' basic habit tracker widget are free to use. The native database route requires manual setup of properties and formulas, while embedding a widget like [Blocs' habit tracker](https://blocs.me/habit-tracker-widget) takes under two minutes with no formulas needed.

### Do I need to know Notion formulas to track habits?

No, not if you use a widget embed. Formulas are only required if you build a custom database with rollups for streak calculations, checkbox properties, and date relations, which is where most DIY habit trackers get abandoned.

### How many habits can I track at once?

With Blocs' free tier you can track a limited number of habits; Blocs Pro removes that cap entirely for unlimited habits, which matters if you're tracking more than a handful of daily routines.

### Will a habit tracker widget slow down my Notion page?

No. Embedded widgets load as lightweight iframes and only render when the page is open, so they don't add meaningful load time to your Notion workspace.

### How long does it take to build a new habit?

Research from Lally et al. in the [European Journal of Social Psychology (2010)](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ejsp.674) found a median of 66 days for a behavior to become automatic, with a range of 18 to 254 days depending on the habit's complexity. Daily tracking helps you stay consistent through that window.

### Can I use a Notion habit tracker on mobile?

Yes. Widget embeds like Blocs' are responsive and work inside the Notion mobile app, so you can check off habits from your phone the same way you would on desktop.
