## Why Doesn't Notion Show Habit Streaks Natively?

Notion is a flexible document and database tool, not a purpose-built habit app. You can create checkbox properties, filter by date, and write formulas to count consecutive completions, but Notion provides no visual streak bar, flame icon, or calendar heatmap out of the box. Getting that requires either a complex Rollup/Formula setup or an external embed that handles the visualization for you.

Most people who try the all-Notion route spend an hour building a database, only to end up with a table of checkboxes. It works, but it doesn't feel motivating. Streak visualization specifically relies on consecutive-day logic that Notion formulas can express but cannot render as an engaging visual.

## How to Embed a Habit Streak Tracker in Notion

Embedding a habit tracker takes under two minutes. You don't install anything or create an account for the free tier.

### Step 1: Open your Notion page

Navigate to the Notion page where you want your habit tracker to live. This could be a daily dashboard, a morning routine page, or a dedicated habits page.

### Step 2: Add an embed block

Type `/embed` and select the **Embed** block. Notion will prompt you for a URL.

### Step 3: Paste the Blocs habit tracker URL

Enter the following URL:

```
https://blocs.me/habit-tracker
```

Hit **Embed link**. The widget loads inline on your page. You'll see a list of habits, a check-in button, and your current streak counter immediately.

### Step 4: Resize to fit your layout

Drag the bottom edge of the embed block to resize. For a sidebar layout, a height of around 400px works well. For a full-width page, give it more room so the analytics panel is fully visible.

### Step 5: Check in daily

Each day you open Notion, mark your habits complete directly in the widget. The streak increments automatically. No external app to open, no separate login.

## Can You Build Habit Streaks Directly in Notion Without a Widget?

Yes, but it's considerably more work. A typical DIY approach involves: creating a Habits database with a checkbox property per habit per day, building a formula that counts consecutive completed days using date arithmetic, and using a gallery or board view to approximate a visual layout. The result is functional but not visually motivating, and it breaks if you miss a day and forget to add a row for it.

For users who enjoy building Notion systems, the database approach is worth exploring. For everyone else, embedding a purpose-built tracker is faster and produces a better visual result. The two approaches aren't mutually exclusive: you can have a Blocs tracker embedded at the top of the same page as your Notion habit database.

### Does Notion have built-in streak tracking?

No. As of August 2026, Notion has no native streak visualization. You can approximate it with date formulas in a database, but there is no built-in streak bar, heatmap, or consecutive-day counter. Embeddable widgets like Blocs fill this gap directly.

### Is the Blocs habit tracker free?

Yes, the basic tier is free with no sign-up required. You get streak tracking and daily check-ins for up to three habits. Blocs Pro ($17 one-time) unlocks unlimited habits, analytics, custom themes, and cloud sync.

### Does the embedded habit tracker work on the Notion mobile app?

Notion's mobile apps support embed blocks, but interactivity can vary depending on your device and Notion version. The Blocs tracker is designed to be touch-friendly. For the most reliable experience, use Notion in a desktop browser.

### Will I lose my streak data if I close Notion?

On the free tier, data is stored locally in your browser session. Closing the tab and reopening it generally preserves your data, but clearing browser storage can reset it. Blocs Pro users get cloud sync, so streaks persist across browsers and devices.

### Can I track multiple habits in the same widget?

Yes. The free tier supports up to three habits in the same widget instance. Blocs Pro removes this limit so you can track as many habits as you want from a single embedded widget.

### Do I need to create an account to use the habit tracker?

No account is needed for the free tier. Just embed the URL in Notion and start tracking. An account is required for Blocs Pro to enable cloud sync and cross-device access.
