## What is a Journal Widget for Notion?

A journal widget for Notion is an embeddable tool — usually delivered as an iframe — that adds interactive, data-driven elements to a Notion page used for daily journaling. Rather than building complex linked databases or installing browser extensions, you paste a URL into Notion's `/embed` block and the widget appears inline, fully interactive. Common examples include habit streak trackers, focus timers, goal progress bars, and daily quote displays.

Notion's own block editor is excellent for writing, but it has no built-in widgets. Third-party embed tools like Blocs fill that gap without forcing you to leave your workspace or manage yet another app.

## How Do You Add a Journal Widget to Notion?

Adding any Blocs widget to your Notion journal takes under two minutes. You don't need to create an account for free widgets — just copy the embed URL and paste it into Notion.

### Step 1: Copy the widget URL

Go to [blocs.me](https://blocs.me) and navigate to the widget page you want (e.g., `blocs.me/habit-tracker-widget`). Copy the embed URL shown on the page (it looks like `https://blocs.me/habit-tracker`).

### Step 2: Open your Notion journal page

Navigate to the Notion page where you want the widget. Click into an empty block and type `/embed`, then press Enter.

### Step 3: Paste the URL

Paste the Blocs widget URL into the embed dialog and click "Embed link." The widget appears inline and is immediately interactive.

### Step 4: Resize as needed

Drag the embed block's bottom edge to adjust height. For side-by-side widgets (e.g., a progress bar next to a habit tracker), use Notion's column layout by dragging one block beside another.
