## Why Doesn't Notion Have a Native Progress Bar?

Notion is a flexible document and database tool, not a purpose-built progress tracker. While you can simulate a progress bar using formula fields, rollup columns, or emoji characters, those approaches require database setup, formula knowledge, and manual upkeep. They also look clunky and don't update visually in real time. For anyone who wants a clean, visual progress indicator without building a spreadsheet, an embedded widget is the practical answer.

## How to Add a Progress Bar to Notion Using Blocs

The Blocs Progress Bar widget embeds directly into Notion as an iframe. Here's the full setup process.

### Step 1: Copy the Embed URL

The Blocs Progress Bar embed URL is:

```
https://blocs.me/progress-bar
```

That's it. No account needed for the basic widget.

### Step 2: Add an Embed Block in Notion

1. Open the Notion page where you want the progress bar.
2. Type **/embed** and select **Embed** from the menu.
3. Paste `https://blocs.me/progress-bar` into the URL field.
4. Click **Embed link**.

### Step 3: Resize to Fit Your Layout

Drag the bottom edge of the embed block to adjust the height. For the Blocs Progress Bar, a height of around 150px and a max width of 500px looks clean on most pages. You can also resize it horizontally by dragging the sides.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Notion support progress bars natively?

Notion does not have a native visual progress bar. You can approximate one with formula fields using emoji characters inside a database, but these require setup knowledge and only work within database views. For a standalone visual bar on any page, an embedded widget like Blocs is the simpler option.

### Does embedding a widget work on the free Notion plan?

Yes. Notion's embed block is available on all plans, including the free plan. You can paste any external URL into an embed block without a paid Notion subscription.
