## Why Add Widgets to Your Notion Budget Template?

A Notion budget template handles the data: income columns, expense categories, monthly totals. But data alone doesn't build the habit of checking it. That's where widgets come in.

Embedding a live habit tracker next to your budget database gives you a daily visual cue to log your expenses. A progress bar showing how close you are to a savings goal makes abstract numbers feel concrete. A pomodoro timer helps you carve out a focused 25-minute window each week for your budget review instead of putting it off.

The key advantage of using Notion widgets versus a standalone budgeting app: everything stays in one place. You don't need to open a separate app, sync data, or remember another login. Your budget template and your productivity tools live on the same Notion page.

## How to Embed a Widget in Your Notion Budget Template

Embedding a Blocs widget in any Notion page takes about 30 seconds:

1. Open your Notion budget template page
2. Type `/embed` and select the Embed block option
3. Paste the widget URL (e.g., `https://blocs.me/habit-tracker`)
4. Click "Embed link" and resize the block to fit your layout
5. Repeat for any additional widgets you want on the page

That's it. No code, no integrations, no third-party accounts required for the free widgets. For Pro widgets, you'll need a Blocs account, which you can set up at [blocs.me/sign-in](https://blocs.me/sign-in).
