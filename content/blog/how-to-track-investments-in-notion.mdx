## What is the best way to track investments in Notion?

The best way is a dedicated Notion database with one row per holding and formula fields that calculate your gain, loss, and allocation automatically. This gives you a single source of truth without a separate spreadsheet, and you can filter or group by account, asset type, or risk level.

### Step-by-step setup

- **Create a new database** (table view works best for this) and title it "Investment Portfolio."
- **Add core properties:** Ticker/Name, Account, Shares, Cost Basis, Purchase Date, Current Price.
- **Add a formula property** for Market Value: `Shares * Current Price`.
- **Add a formula property** for Gain/Loss %: `(Market Value - (Shares * Cost Basis)) / (Shares * Cost Basis)`.
- **Add a rollup** at the top (or a linked summary view) that sums Market Value across all rows for your total portfolio value.
- **Group by Account or Asset Type** to see allocation across brokerages, retirement accounts, or crypto wallets.

This native approach costs nothing and lives entirely inside your existing Notion workspace, so you're not bouncing between a brokerage app, a budgeting app, and Notion just to see where you stand.

## Can Notion automatically update stock prices?

No, not by default. Notion's formula and rollup system (documented at [notion.so](https://www.notion.so)) can calculate values from numbers you enter, but it has no built-in connection to live market data. Most people either update "Current Price" manually once a week or wire up a third-party automation (like Make.com or a custom API script) that writes prices into the database on a schedule.

For most retail investors checking in weekly rather than day-trading, manual updates are fine, and it forces a regular review habit rather than compulsive refreshing.

## FAQs

### What formula tracks investment gains in Notion?

Use `(Market Value - (Shares * Cost Basis)) / (Shares * Cost Basis)` as a formula property to calculate percentage gain or loss per holding, then format the property as a percent.

### Can I track crypto alongside stocks in the same Notion database?

Yes. Add an "Asset Type" select property (Stock, Crypto, ETF, Bond) to the same database so you can filter and group holdings by type while keeping one unified portfolio view.

### Is there a way to automate stock price updates in Notion?

Only through third-party automation tools (like Make.com or a custom script using Notion's API) that write prices into your database on a schedule. Notion has no native live market-data connector.

### Should I use a Notion investment template or build my own?

Either works. A template saves setup time, but a custom database lets you match the exact properties (accounts, asset types, formulas) you actually use, which matters more once your portfolio has multiple brokerages or account types.
