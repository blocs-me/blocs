## What should you track for mental health in Notion?

Track the few signals that actually change your behavior, not everything you can think of. Overloading a tracker with 15 metrics is the fastest way to abandon it within a week.

- **Daily mood check-in** - a one-tap log, even without a 1-10 scale, builds the habit of pausing to notice how you feel.
- **Therapy or medication adherence** - simple yes/no logging catches missed sessions or doses before they become a pattern.
- **Sleep and water intake** - both are tightly linked to mood regulation; Blocs' free [Water Tracker](https://blocs.me/water-tracker-widget) covers hydration specifically.
- **Gratitude or journaling** - the [APA notes](https://www.apa.org/topics/journaling) that brief daily journaling is associated with reduced anxiety symptoms over time.
- **Social connection or self-care actions** - things like "called a friend" or "went outside" are easy wins to log and reinforce.
