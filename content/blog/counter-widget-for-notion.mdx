## What Is a Counter Widget for Notion?

A counter widget for Notion is an embeddable tool that lets you increment, track, or visualize a running count directly inside a Notion page. Unlike a simple Notion database number field, a dedicated counter widget gives you a visual UI, tap-to-increment interactions, daily goals, and historical data — all without leaving your workspace.

Notion's native embed block (`/embed`) supports iframes, which means any widget hosted at a public URL can live inside your pages. This approach gives you real interactivity that plain databases can't match: colored progress rings, streak counters, unit selectors, and animated feedback when you hit a goal.

## How to Embed a Counter Widget in Notion?

Embedding any Blocs counter widget in Notion takes under two minutes and requires no account for the free widgets. Copy the widget URL from blocs.me, open your Notion page, type `/embed`, and paste the URL.

### Step-by-step instructions

1. Go to the widget page on [blocs.me](https://blocs.me) (e.g., `blocs.me/water-tracker` or `blocs.me/habit-tracker`)
2. Copy the embed URL shown on that page
3. Open your Notion page and type `/embed` in an empty block
4. Paste the URL and press **Embed link**
5. Resize the embed block by dragging the edges to fit your layout

For best results, embed the widget full-column-width or in a two-column layout alongside your notes. Notion remembers your embed size, so you only need to resize once.

## Why Not Just Use a Notion Database as a Counter?

Notion databases with number fields can technically store a count, but they don't give you a visual counter experience. You'd need to manually type in a new number each day, write formulas to calculate streaks, and build rollup views to see trends — that's significant setup for something a dedicated widget handles out of the box. A purpose-built counter widget also gives you tap/click interaction, animated fill states, and mobile-friendly touch targets that a Notion table cell simply can't match.
