## What Is a Google Calendar Widget for Notion?

A Google Calendar widget for Notion is any embed or integration that surfaces your Google Calendar events directly on a Notion page. Because Notion supports `/embed` blocks, you can paste an iframe URL into a page and display content from external sources, including Google Calendar's public embed link.

The result looks like a calendar inside your Notion workspace, but it behaves like a web page view, not a live integration. You can see events, but you can't create or edit them from within Notion. For many users, this is enough. For others, it's frustrating.

## How to Embed Google Calendar in Notion (Free Method)

This is the simplest approach and costs nothing. It works by using Google Calendar's built-in "publish and embed" feature to generate an iframe URL, then pasting that into a Notion embed block.

### Step 1: Get your Google Calendar embed link

Open [Google Calendar](https://calendar.google.com) on desktop. Go to **Settings** (gear icon) then select the specific calendar you want to share. Scroll to the **Integrate calendar** section. Copy the **Embed code** — it will look like an iframe tag with a long URL inside the `src` attribute. Copy just the URL portion (starting with `https://calendar.google.com/calendar/embed?...`).

### Step 2: Make the calendar public

For the embed to work, the calendar needs to be set to public. Under **Access permissions** on the same settings page, check "Make available to public." Note that this means anyone with the link can view your events, so avoid doing this for calendars containing sensitive information.

### Step 3: Embed it in Notion

Open the Notion page where you want the calendar. Type `/embed` and select the Embed block. Paste the Google Calendar URL into the prompt and press Enter. Resize the embed block to your liking. The calendar will render inline on the page.

### Limitations of the free Google Calendar embed

- **Read-only:** You cannot create, edit, or delete events from within Notion
- **No mobile support:** The Google Calendar embed doesn't render well on mobile devices or the Notion mobile app
- **Styling mismatch:** The Google Calendar UI doesn't adapt to Notion's dark mode or your page theme
- **Privacy trade-off:** The calendar must be set to public for the embed to function
- **Refresh lag:** The embed can take a few minutes to reflect newly added events

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I sync Google Calendar with Notion for free?

Not with full two-way sync, no. The free Google Calendar embed shows your events as a read-only view. For true sync where events become Notion database records, you need a tool like Zapier, which has its own paid plan. Blocs offers a visual calendar widget as a polished alternative for users who just need to see a calendar inside Notion.

### Does the Google Calendar embed work on Notion mobile?

No. Notion's embed block on mobile renders a static image or a link to the embedded page rather than an interactive iframe. The Google Calendar embed doesn't function interactively on the Notion mobile app. The Blocs Calendar Widget is designed to be mobile-friendly.

### Is the Blocs Calendar Widget a Google Calendar replacement?

It's a visual calendar for your Notion workspace, not a Google Calendar integration. It won't pull in your existing Google Calendar events. It's best for users who want a clean calendar display or date-tracking widget embedded in their Notion pages, not for those who need to see their Google Calendar appointments.

This is a solid free option if your goal is to visualize tasks or events already stored in a Notion database. It doesn't connect to Google Calendar, but for Notion-native workflows, it's often the right tool for the job.
