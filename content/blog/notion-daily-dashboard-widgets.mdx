## What Makes a Good Notion Daily Dashboard Widget?

A daily dashboard widget needs to do three things well: load instantly inside Notion, update in real time, and actually change your behavior. Decorative embeds that sit static on a page add visual noise without utility. The widgets worth including on a daily Notion dashboard are ones you interact with every single day.

The most useful categories for a daily setup:

- **Time management**: Pomodoro timers, countdown timers, clock widgets
- **Health and habit loops**: Habit trackers, water intake trackers
- **Ambient awareness**: Live weather, quotes, visual calendar
- **Progress visibility**: Progress bars, streak counters

Each of these maps to a real daily friction point. Without them, Notion users typically rely on phone apps for timers, separate habit-tracking apps, and weather apps they have to switch to manually. Embedding these tools directly into your Notion daily dashboard removes that context-switching entirely.

## How to Add Widgets to Your Notion Daily Dashboard

### Step 1: Get your widget URL

Navigate to the Blocs widget you want (for example, `https://blocs.me/pomodoro-timer`) and copy the embed URL shown on that page. Free widgets work immediately with no account required.

### Step 2: Open your Notion daily page

Open the Notion page where you want the widget. Type `/embed` in a new block to insert an embed block.

### Step 3: Paste the embed URL

Paste the Blocs widget URL into the embed field and click "Embed link." The widget will render live inside your Notion page, interactive and fully functional.

### Step 4: Resize and position

Drag the edges of the embed block to resize. Use Notion's column layout to place multiple widgets side by side: a Pomodoro timer on the left, a habit tracker in the middle, and a water tracker on the right works well for a compact daily header.
