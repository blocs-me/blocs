## What is a savings tracker for Notion?

A savings tracker for Notion is any tool, template, or widget that shows how close you are to a money goal directly inside your Notion workspace. Most people build this manually with a database and rollup formulas, or embed a visual widget that updates as you check off progress, avoiding the need to maintain spreadsheet-style formulas.

The manual database approach (a table with "Target," "Saved," and a formula calculating percentage) is flexible but takes setup time and breaks easily if you reformat the database. A widget embed skips that setup entirely, which is why many Notion users search for a pre-built option instead.
