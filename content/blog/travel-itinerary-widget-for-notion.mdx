## Is there a travel itinerary widget for Notion?

Not as a standalone embeddable widget as of November 2026. Travel itineraries in Notion are typically built as a database (table or gallery view) with columns for date, location, confirmation numbers, and notes, because the information is structured and tabular rather than a live, ticking piece of UI. Widgets like [Blocs Calendar](https://blocs.me/calendar-widget) complement that database by giving you a visual, at-a-glance view of trip dates without leaving your [Notion](https://blocs.me) page.

## How do you build a trip itinerary in Notion today?

Create a database with properties for date, city, activity, and booking reference, then embed a Calendar widget above it so trip dates are visible without opening the database. Add a Countdown Timer at the top of the page so collaborators see days-until-departure at a glance.

### Step-by-step setup

- Create a new Notion page for the trip and add a database (table view) with date, location, activity, and confirmation columns.
- Embed a [Calendar widget](https://blocs.me/calendar-widget) at the top to visualize flight dates, hotel check-ins, and activities.
- Add a [Countdown Timer](https://blocs.me/countdown-timer) for "days until departure" so the urgency is visible at a glance. See our [guide on adding a countdown timer to Notion](https://blocs.me/blog/how-to-add-a-countdown-timer-to-notion) for setup details.
- If the trip involves a daily routine (workouts, journaling, budget check-ins), add a [Habit Tracker](https://blocs.me/habit-tracker-widget) to keep it consistent while traveling.
