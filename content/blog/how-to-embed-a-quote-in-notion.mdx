## How to Embed a Quote in Notion

Notion supports embedding any publicly accessible URL as an iframe block. The Blocs Quote of the Day widget is designed specifically for this. Here's the exact process:

### Step 1: Open Your Notion Page

Navigate to the Notion page where you want the quote to appear. This works on any page type: a dashboard, daily journal, morning routine template, or a standalone page dedicated to inspiration.

### Step 2: Add an Embed Block

Click on a new line, type `/embed`, and select the **Embed** block option from the menu. Notion will display a text field asking for a URL.

### Step 3: Paste the Blocs Widget URL

Paste the following URL into the embed field and press **Enter**:

```
https://blocs.me/quote
```

Notion will render the widget inline on your page. You'll see a Quote of the Day card that refreshes automatically each day.

### Step 4: Resize to Fit

Grab the edges of the embed block to resize it. The widget adapts to different widths, so you can make it compact for a sidebar column or wider for a hero section on your dashboard.

## What Are the Alternatives to Embedding a Quote Widget?

There are a few approaches people try before landing on a dedicated widget. Each has real drawbacks:

- **Manual copy-paste into a callout block:** You have to update it yourself every day. It stops being "daily inspiration" and starts being a chore.
- **A Notion database with a gallery view:** Requires building and populating the database, setting up filters, and the result is a static list rather than a live feed.
- **Third-party automation tools (e.g., Zapier + Notion API):** Technically possible, but requires API access (Notion Plus plan or higher), workflow setup, and ongoing maintenance. Far more complexity than the problem warrants.
- **A separate quotes app:** Forces you to switch context outside Notion, which defeats the purpose of a centralized workspace.

A widget like Blocs solves this cleanly: one URL, one embed block, zero ongoing maintenance.

## FAQs

### Does Notion support embedding iframes?

Notion supports embedding any publicly accessible URL using the **/embed** block. The URL must point to a page that allows iframe embedding. Blocs widgets are configured specifically to work with Notion's embed system.

Yes. The Blocs Quote widget refreshes once per day without any action required from you. Open your Notion page each morning and the quote will already be updated.

### Does this work on the free Notion plan?

Yes. Notion's embed block is available on all plans, including the free tier. The Blocs Quote widget itself is also free with no sign-up required for the basic version.

### Can I choose the type of quotes shown?

### Will the widget work in the Notion mobile app?

Embed blocks in Notion are supported on desktop (web and desktop app). Mobile support for embeds in Notion can be inconsistent depending on the app version and device. For the best experience, the widget is recommended for desktop use.

### Can I add multiple quote widgets to the same page?
