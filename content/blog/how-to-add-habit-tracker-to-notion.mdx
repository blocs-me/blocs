## Why Not Just Use a Notion Database?

Notion databases can technically track habits, but the setup is tedious. You need checkbox properties for each habit, formula properties for streak counts, filtered views to see today's habits, and rollup properties if you want weekly summaries. Every new habit means schema changes. And if you want a visual progress indicator or streak counter, you are writing formulas that break whenever Notion updates its formula syntax.

An embedded widget skips all of that. It handles the UI, the logic, and the data persistence on its own. You get a purpose-built tool that slots into your existing Notion workspace without touching your database structure.

## How to Add a Habit Tracker to Notion (Step by Step)

Embedding the Blocs [habit tracker widget](https://blocs.me/habit-tracker-widget) takes two steps. You do not need an account, and you do not need to install anything.

### Step 1: Copy the Embed URL

The embed URL for the Blocs habit tracker is:

`https://blocs.me/habit-tracker`

Copy that URL. That is all you need.

### Step 2: Embed It in Notion

1. Open the Notion page where you want your habit tracker
2. Type **/embed** and select the Embed block from the menu
3. Paste `https://blocs.me/habit-tracker` into the URL field
4. Click **Embed link**

The widget loads inline. You can resize it by dragging the edges. Your habits and check-ins are stored automatically. Come back tomorrow and your data will still be there.

### Step 3: Add Your Habits

Once embedded, the widget lets you name your habits directly in the interface. Free users can track up to 3 habits. Check them off daily by clicking the habit row. The widget marks each day as complete and starts building your streak automatically.
