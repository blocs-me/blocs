## What is a Notion Tracker Widget?

A Notion tracker widget is an interactive embed — typically an iframe — placed directly on a Notion page to let you log habits, water intake, goals, or progress without leaving your workspace. Unlike manual Notion databases or separate apps, a good tracker widget updates in real time, persists your data across sessions, and requires zero database setup.

Most Notion users start by building tracker tables or toggle lists manually. The problem is that these require daily upkeep, offer no visual streak tracking, and clutter your pages with complex database views. A purpose-built widget handles all of that automatically.

## How to Embed a Tracker Widget in Notion?

Embedding a Blocs tracker widget in Notion takes under 60 seconds. You don't need a developer, an API key, or a Notion integration. Here's the full process:

1. **Copy the widget URL.** Each widget has its own embed URL: `https://blocs.me/habit-tracker`, `https://blocs.me/water-tracker`, or `https://blocs.me/progress-bar`.
2. **Open your Notion page.** Navigate to the page where you want the tracker to live.
3. **Add an Embed block.** Type `/embed` in Notion, select the Embed block, and paste the widget URL.
4. **Resize as needed.** Drag the embed block to your preferred height. The widget is responsive and adapts to the container.
5. **Start tracking.** The widget loads immediately, remembers your data, and syncs across devices if you're on Pro.
