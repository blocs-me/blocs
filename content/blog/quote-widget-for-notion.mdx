## What is a Quote Widget for Notion?

A quote widget for Notion is an embeddable element that displays an inspirational or motivational quote directly inside a Notion page. Instead of switching to a separate app or manually copying quotes into a text block, the widget updates automatically — usually on a daily cycle — keeping your workspace fresh without any extra effort.

Notion doesn't include a native quote-of-the-day feature. Its built-in **Quote block** is static; you write text into it once and it stays there. A proper quote widget fetches new quotes dynamically and renders them as a styled card inside your page, making your dashboard feel alive rather than frozen.

This is especially popular among students, writers, and anyone who uses Notion as a daily home base. A well-placed quote at the top of a dashboard or journal page sets a tone for the day without requiring you to open another app.
