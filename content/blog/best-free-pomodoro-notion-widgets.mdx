## What is a pomodoro Notion widget?

A pomodoro Notion widget is an embeddable timer that runs the pomodoro technique (25 minutes of focused work followed by a 5-minute break) directly inside a Notion page, instead of requiring a separate app or browser tab. It's added via Notion's embed block using an iframe URL, so it updates and runs live wherever you place it.

The technique itself comes from developer Francesco Cirillo, who popularized the 25/5 work-rest cycle in the late 1980s as a way to structure focused work into measurable intervals ([The Pomodoro Technique, Francesco Cirillo](https://francescocirillo.com/pages/pomodoro-technique)). Notion widgets bring that same structure into a workspace people already use for notes, tasks, and planning.
