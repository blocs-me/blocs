## How to Add a Quote of the Day Widget to Notion

The embed process takes under a minute. Here are the steps:

### Step 1: Copy the widget URL

Go to [blocs.me/quote-widget](https://blocs.me/quote-widget) and copy the embed URL for the quote widget. It will look like `https://blocs.me/quote`.

### Step 2: Open your Notion page

Navigate to any Notion page where you want the quote to live. A dashboard, a daily journal template, or a home page all work well.

### Step 3: Add an Embed block

Type `/embed` in Notion and select the Embed block option from the menu that appears.

### Step 4: Paste the URL

Paste the Blocs widget URL into the embed field and click Embed link. The quote widget will appear inline on your page immediately.

### Step 5: Resize to fit

Drag the edges of the embed block to adjust its size. The widget scales cleanly at most widths, though a narrower column tends to look best for this type of widget.

That is the entire setup. From this point on the widget updates itself. You will see a new quote every day without touching anything.
