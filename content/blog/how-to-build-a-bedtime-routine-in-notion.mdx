## What is a bedtime routine setup in Notion?

A Notion bedtime routine is a dedicated page or database that lists the steps you follow before sleep each night, paired with a way to check them off and track consistency over time. Most setups use either a Notion to-do database (manual, flexible) or an embedded widget (visual, less setup) to log completion. The two approaches aren't mutually exclusive, many people use both.

### Option 1: Notion checklist database

Create a database with columns for Date, Task, and Done. List your routine steps (e.g., "Phone away by 9:30," "Stretch 10 min," "Lights out by 10:30") as recurring tasks using Notion's template button or a simple relation to a master list. This gives full control over task wording but requires manual setup for recurring entries and offers no visual streak display out of the box.

## How do you set up a bedtime routine in Notion step by step?

Set up a Notion bedtime routine by creating a dedicated "Night Routine" page, listing 4-6 concrete steps, embedding a habit tracker widget below the list, and checking it off at the same time each night for at least two weeks to build consistency.

1. **Create a new page** titled "Night Routine" or add a section to your existing daily planner.
2. **List your steps**: keep it to 4-6 items max. Example: dim lights, no screens 30 min before bed, hydrate earlier (not right before sleep), journal or read, lights out by a fixed time.
3. **Embed a habit tracker** below the list using the `/embed` command and the widget URL above.
4. **Add a water tracker if hydration timing matters to your routine** (many sleep guides recommend stopping fluids 1-2 hours before bed to avoid night waking, per the [Sleep Foundation](https://www.sleepfoundation.org/sleep-hygiene/bedtime-routines-for-adults)).
5. **Check in nightly** and let the streak counter do the motivational work instead of relying on memory.

## Why add a water tracker to a sleep routine?

A water tracker helps you front-load hydration earlier in the day so you're not drinking large amounts right before bed, which can cause nighttime waking. Logging water intake alongside your bedtime checklist makes the connection between daytime habits and sleep quality visible in one place.
