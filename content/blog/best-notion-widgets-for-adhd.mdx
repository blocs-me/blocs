## What makes a Notion widget ADHD-friendly?

An ADHD-friendly Notion widget stays visible on the page, gives immediate visual feedback, and doesn't require extra clicks to check progress. Standard Notion databases force you to open a view to see status, which is an extra decision point that ADHD brains often skip entirely under executive-function load.

Clinicians and ADHD coaches consistently point to external, visible cues as a workaround for the internal working-memory and time-perception challenges common in ADHD, often called "time blindness" ([CHADD](https://chadd.org/for-adults/time-management/)). A widget that's always on screen functions as that external cue without you having to build or remember a system.
