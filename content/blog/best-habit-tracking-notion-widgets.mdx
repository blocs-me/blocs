## What is a habit tracking Notion widget?

A habit tracking Notion widget is an embeddable tool, usually added via the Notion `/embed` block, that displays a visual habit grid or checklist directly inside a Notion page. Unlike a native Notion database, it requires no table setup, formulas, or rollups — you paste a URL and the widget renders as a live, interactive iframe.
