## How to Add a Water Tracker Widget to Notion

Embedding the Blocs water tracker takes three steps and less than a minute. No coding, no Notion API access required.

1. **Open your Notion page** — navigate to any page where you want the tracker to live (a daily dashboard, a health page, a morning routine template).
2. **Type `/embed`** — select the "Embed" block from the slash command menu.
3. **Paste the widget URL** — enter `https://blocs.me/water-tracker` and click "Embed link".

Notion will render the widget inline. Resize the embed block by dragging its edges to fit your layout. For a full walkthrough, see the guide on [how to track daily water intake in Notion](https://blocs.me/blog/how-to-track-daily-water-intake-in-notion).
