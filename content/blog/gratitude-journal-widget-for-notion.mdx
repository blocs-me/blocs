## Why does gratitude journaling work?

Gratitude journaling works because consistent, brief daily reflection reinforces a habit loop: a small visual streak indicator makes the behavior easier to repeat, which is the same mechanism behind any habit-tracking tool. Psychologist Robert Emmons, a leading researcher on gratitude at UC Davis, has published widely cited work arguing that regularly recording things you're grateful for is associated with improved mood and well-being over time. The tracking piece (a visible streak) is what most people struggle to sustain without a tool - a widget just removes that friction.
