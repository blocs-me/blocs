## How to Add a Workout Tracker to Notion

### Step 1: Open your Notion fitness page

Create a new Notion page or open an existing one for your workout dashboard. A dedicated page works best — you'll be embedding a few blocks.

### Step 2: Copy the Blocs habit tracker URL

Go to [blocs.me/habit-tracker-widget](https://blocs.me/habit-tracker-widget) and copy the embed URL: `https://blocs.me/habit-tracker`.

### Step 3: Embed in Notion

In your Notion page, type `/embed`, select the Embed block, and paste the URL. Resize the block to fit your layout. The widget loads immediately — no sign-in required for the free tier.

### Step 4: Configure your habit

Rename your habit to "Workout" or "Gym" inside the widget. Free users get one default habit. With [Blocs Pro](https://blocs.me/pricing), you can track multiple habits (e.g., "Strength", "Cardio", "Stretch") and unlock weekly and monthly analytics with streak tracking.

### Step 5: Add a progress bar (optional but recommended)

Repeat the embed process with `https://blocs.me/progress-bar`. Set your annual goal (e.g., 150 workouts) and update it after each session. The bar fills visually as you progress — a simple motivator that a Notion formula column can't replicate.
