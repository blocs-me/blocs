## How do I set up a weekly planner in Notion?

Create a new page, add a toggle list for "This Week's Priorities" (3-5 items max), then add a simple table or heading-per-day layout for scheduling. Embed a timer and habit tracker widget below it so the whole plan lives on one page instead of scattered across templates.

### Step-by-step setup

- **Create the page.** Title it with the week's date range so old plans stay searchable.
- **Add 3-5 priorities.** Anything more and you're making a task dump, not a weekly plan.
- **Block your days.** Use H3 headings for each day (Mon-Sun) with a bullet list of time slots underneath.
- **Embed a timer widget** for focused work sessions directly on the page (see below).
- **Add a habit or review tracker** at the bottom to close the loop each day.

## FAQs

### Can I plan my week in Notion for free?

Yes. Notion's free plan covers unlimited pages, and Blocs' Pomodoro Timer and Habit Tracker widgets are free to embed with no sign-up, covering the core needs of time-blocking and habit tracking for weekly planning.

### Do I need a template to plan my week in Notion?

No. A single blank page with priorities, daily headings, and an embedded timer or habit widget is enough. Templates help if you want pre-built structure, but they're not required to plan effectively.

### What's the difference between time-blocking and a to-do list?

A to-do list just names tasks; time-blocking assigns each task a specific slot on your calendar or page. Time-blocking forces realistic planning because you can only fit as much as actually fits in your week.

### How do I review my week in Notion?

Add a short "Weekly Review" toggle at the bottom of your plan page with 2-3 questions (what worked, what didn't, next week's priority). Pair it with habit tracker data if you want to see completion trends over time.

### Are Notion widgets safe to embed?

Yes, widgets like those from Blocs run as standard iframes inside your Notion page, the same embed method Notion uses for YouTube or Google Maps. No data entered in Notion is shared with the widget beyond what you input directly in it.

### Can I use multiple widgets on one weekly plan?

Yes. You can embed a Pomodoro Timer, Habit Tracker, and Water Tracker on the same page since each is an independent iframe. There's no limit to how many you combine on a single weekly planning page.
