## What is a weekly planner widget for Notion?

A weekly planner widget is an embeddable tool that shows a 7-day view of tasks, habits, or events directly inside a Notion page, instead of requiring a separate database, template, or linked view. It's rendered as an iframe, so it updates live without you rebuilding Notion's native calendar or table views.

## How do you build a weekly planner in Notion with widgets?

Embed the Calendar widget for your week-at-a-glance view, then embed the Habit Tracker directly below it for recurring weekly commitments (workouts, reading, deep work blocks). Both update independently and don't require any Notion database setup.

1. Go to [blocs.me/calendar-widget](https://blocs.me/calendar-widget), customize the date markers and theme
2. Copy the generated embed link
3. In Notion, type `/embed` and paste the link
4. Repeat with [blocs.me/habit-tracker-widget](https://blocs.me/habit-tracker-widget) for weekly habits
5. Resize both blocks side-by-side for a true "weekly planner" layout

## What are the alternatives to a dedicated weekly planner widget?

Most "weekly planner" solutions for Notion fall into three categories: manually built databases with weekly rollup views, downloadable templates that require duplication and customization, or third-party widget embeds similar to Blocs, Indify, or Apption. The database route is the most flexible but takes the longest to set up and maintain; template galleries are fast but static and don't update live; widget embeds are the quickest to deploy but vary widely in design quality and whether they're free or subscription-based.

| Approach | Setup time | Live updates | Cost model |
|---|---|---|---|
| Native Notion database | 30-60+ min | Yes | Free (your time) |
| Downloaded template | 5-10 min | No (static) | Often paid templates |
| Blocs Calendar + Habit Tracker | Under 2 min | Yes | Free tier available; Pro is $17 one-time |
| Other widget embed tools | Varies | Yes | Often monthly subscriptions |
