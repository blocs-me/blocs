## What is a Date Widget for Notion?

A date widget for Notion is an embeddable element that displays date-related information visually inside a Notion page. This includes countdown timers that tick down to a deadline, interactive monthly calendars, progress bars tied to a date range, or a live clock. Because Notion's native date fields are functional but not visual, widgets fill the gap for anyone who wants to *see* time passing or approaching — not just read a timestamp in a database cell.

The most common types people search for:

- **Countdown timer** — shows days, hours, minutes until a specific date
- **Calendar widget** — interactive monthly grid, optionally synced with Google Calendar
- **Date progress bar** — visualizes how far through a date range you are (e.g., a project sprint)
- **Live clock** — shows the current date and time, sometimes as a flip clock

## How to Add a Date Widget to Notion?

Adding any Blocs widget to Notion takes under two minutes. The process is identical for all widget types — countdown, calendar, clock, or progress bar.

1. **Go to the widget page.** Visit the relevant Blocs widget page (e.g., [blocs.me/countdown-timer](https://blocs.me/countdown-timer)).
2. **Configure your widget.** Set your target date, theme, label, and any other options. Pro users get full customization.
3. **Copy the embed URL.** Click the "Copy embed link" button to get the iframe URL.
4. **Open your Notion page.** In any Notion page, type `/embed` and press Enter to insert an embed block.
5. **Paste the URL.** Paste the copied URL into the embed dialog and confirm. The widget appears live on the page.
6. **Resize as needed.** Drag the embed block handles to adjust the widget's width and height on the page.

No Notion account upgrade is needed to embed widgets. The embed block is available on all Notion plans, including the free tier. For a more detailed walkthrough, see the [calendar widget for Notion guide](https://blocs.me/blog/calendar-widget-for-notion).
