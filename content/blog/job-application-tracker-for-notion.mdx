## What is the best job application tracker for Notion?

The best current setup combines a Notion database (to log company, role, status, and dates) with embedded Blocs widgets that visualize progress: a Habit Tracker for "applied today" streaks, a Progress Bar for monthly application quotas, and a Calendar for interview scheduling. This beats a plain database alone because it adds at-a-glance motivation without leaving Notion.

### Why not just use a Notion database by itself?

A database (table or board view) is the backbone of any tracker and works fine on its own for logging data. The problem is databases don't give you a quick visual signal of momentum - you have to open filters and sort views to see how you're doing. Widgets solve that by sitting at the top of the page as a persistent, glanceable summary.

## How do you set up a job application tracker in Notion?

Create a Notion database with columns for Company, Role, Status, Date Applied, and Interview Date, then embed three Blocs widgets above it: Habit Tracker for a daily "applied today" check-in, Progress Bar for a monthly goal, and Calendar for upcoming interviews. Each widget is a single iframe embed, so no plugins or integrations are required.

### Step 1: Build the database

- Add a new full-page database with a Board view grouped by Status (Applied, Interview, Offer, Rejected).
- Columns: Company (title), Role (text), Status (select), Date Applied (date), Interview Date (date), Notes (text).

### Step 2: Add the Habit Tracker for daily consistency

Job searching stalls when there's no daily accountability. Set up a "Submit 1+ application" habit in the free Habit Tracker so you get a visible streak every day you apply - the same mechanism people use for [habit tracking in Notion](https://blocs.me/blog/best-habit-tracking-notion-widgets) generally.

## FAQs

### Can I track interview stages visually in Notion?

Yes - use a Board view grouped by Status (Applied, Phone Screen, Interview, Offer, Rejected) so cards move across stages as you update them, and add the Calendar widget on top to surface interview dates without opening the board.

### How many job applications should I set as a monthly goal?

There's no universal number since it depends on your field and how targeted your applications are, but many career coaches suggest setting a consistent weekly volume (e.g., 10-15 applications) rather than a single large monthly figure, so you keep a steady weekly application habit going.

### Does this setup sync across devices?

The Notion database syncs natively through your Notion account. Blocs widget data (habit streaks, progress bar values) syncs across devices only on the Pro plan; the free tier stores data locally to the browser/page.

### Can I customize the colors of the Habit Tracker or Progress Bar to match my job search board?

Yes, on Blocs Pro you can customize widget themes and colors so they match the rest of your Notion workspace, including the job tracker page.

### What if I want reminders to apply every day?

Blocs widgets don't send push notifications; the Habit Tracker's visual streak is the accountability mechanism. For reminders, pair it with Notion's own reminder feature on a linked task.
