## What is a pregnancy tracker for Notion?

A pregnancy tracker for Notion is a page or widget that shows how far along a pregnancy is, how many days remain until the due date, and daily wellness habits like water intake or vitamins. Most people build this with a Notion database template, but embeddable widgets do the same job with less setup and real-time visuals.

Standard Notion database templates require manually building formulas to calculate weeks and days, which breaks easily if you change a date field. Widgets avoid this because the countdown and progress math run automatically in the embed.

## How many weeks is a full-term pregnancy?

A full-term pregnancy is typically counted as 40 weeks from the first day of the last menstrual period, divided into three trimesters of roughly 13 weeks each. This is the standard figure used by the [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/pregnancy/during.html) and most prenatal care providers, and it's the number you should set as the goal in a Progress Bar widget.
