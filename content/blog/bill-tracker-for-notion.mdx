## What is a bill tracker for Notion?

A bill tracker for Notion is a database or widget that lists recurring payments (rent, subscriptions, utilities) along with due dates, amounts, and payment status, so you can see at a glance what's paid and what's coming up. Notion has no native bill-tracking template built in, which is why most people assemble one from a database plus formulas, or embed a third-party widget for visual reminders.

## How do you track bills in Notion?

Track bills in Notion by creating a database with properties for bill name, amount, due date, recurrence, and a checkbox or status field for "paid." Use a formula property to flag overdue items in red, and a linked calendar view to see due dates by month. For visual daily reminders, embed a widget like Blocs' Habit Tracker alongside the database.

### Step-by-step setup

1. **Create the database.** Add properties: Bill Name (title), Amount (number), Due Date (date), Recurrence (select: weekly/monthly/yearly), and Status (checkbox or select).
2. **Add a formula for overdue flags.** Use `if(and(not(Status), Due Date < now()), "Overdue", "On track")` so late bills visually stand out. Notion's own guide to relations and rollups explains how to build these kinds of conditional formulas ([Notion Help: Relations & Rollups](https://www.notion.so/help/relations-and-rollups)).
3. **Add a calendar view** filtered by Due Date so bills show up on a monthly grid.
4. **Embed a reminder widget.** Since bills are recurring, treat each bill category as a "habit" (e.g., "Pay rent," "Pay internet") inside the [Blocs Habit Tracker](/habit-tracker-widget) so you get a visual streak of on-time payments next to your database.

No. Notion has no native bill-tracking feature, but its flexible database system lets you build one using date, number, and formula properties, or by installing a community template from the [Notion template gallery](https://www.notion.so/templates).

### Is there a free bill tracker widget for Notion?

There's no dedicated free bill-tracker widget yet from Blocs, but the free [Habit Tracker widget](/habit-tracker-widget) can be repurposed to check off recurring bills, and it requires no sign-up.

### How do I embed a tracker widget in Notion?

Copy the widget's embed URL (for example blocs.me/habit-tracker), paste it into a Notion page, and select "Embed" from the link preview menu. The widget then renders live inside your workspace.
