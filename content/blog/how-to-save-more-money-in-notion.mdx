## How to Build a Budget Tracker in Notion

A Notion budget tracker is a database with one row per transaction. Add properties for date, category (food, rent, transport, subscriptions), amount, and type (income or expense). A formula property calculates running totals. This single database is the backbone of your money-saving system in Notion.

### Step 1: Create Your Transactions Database

1. Open a new Notion page. Type `/database` and choose **Table - Full page**.
2. Rename it "Budget Tracker".
3. Add these properties: **Date** (Date), **Description** (Text), **Category** (Select — add options: Housing, Food, Transport, Entertainment, Savings, Income), **Amount** (Number, formatted as currency), **Type** (Select: Income / Expense).
4. Add a **Formula** property called "Signed Amount": `if(prop("Type") == "Income", prop("Amount"), -prop("Amount"))`. This lets you sum easily in a rollup or filtered view.

### Step 2: Create Monthly Views

1. Add a **Filter** to show only the current month (Date is within "this month").
2. Duplicate that view for each month you want to track, adjusting the date filter.
3. Use the **Calculate** footer row to show the Sum of your Signed Amount column — this gives you net savings at a glance.

### Step 3: Add a Savings Goals Page

Create a separate Notion page (or sub-page) called "Savings Goals". Add a database with columns for Goal Name, Target Amount, Current Amount, and a Formula for percentage complete: `round(prop("Current Amount") / prop("Target Amount") * 100)`. Update the Current Amount each time you move money to savings.

## FAQs: Saving Money in Notion

### Can Notion replace a budgeting app?

For manual budgeters, yes. Notion handles transaction logging, category breakdowns, savings goals, and recurring expense tracking through databases and formulas. It lacks automatic bank sync, which dedicated apps like YNAB or Copilot offer. If you prefer hands-on control and already use Notion daily, it's a capable replacement with no extra subscription cost.

### Does Notion have a built-in budget template?

Notion's template gallery includes several community-built budget templates. These are good starting points, but most need customization to match your income structure and spending categories. Building from scratch (as described above) often results in a simpler, more personal setup that you'll actually maintain.

### How do I add a progress bar to my savings goal in Notion?

Notion's native formula column can display a text-based progress bar, but for a visual graphic bar you need an embedded widget. Use the [Blocs Progress Bar](https://blocs.me/progress-bar-widget) (Pro): copy the embed URL, type `/embed` in Notion, paste it, and resize. It renders as a live, styled progress bar inside your page.

### What habits should I track to save money?

The most effective money habits to track daily are: logging transactions (keeps you honest), packing lunch or cooking at home, checking your budget balance, and transferring a fixed amount to savings. Tracking the behavior — not just the outcome — is what makes the habit stick, according to [behavioral research on habit formation](https://jamesclear.com/habit-tracking).

### How often should I update my Notion budget?

Daily updates take 2-3 minutes and produce the best results. Weekly updates work but create a longer gap between spending and awareness. The habit tracker check-in ("Did I log transactions today?") is a good prompt to keep the cadence consistent.
