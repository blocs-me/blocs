## How Do You Embed Widgets in Notion?

Embedding a Blocs widget in Notion takes under a minute:

1. Copy the widget URL (e.g., `https://blocs.me/pomodoro`)
2. In Notion, type `/embed` and press Enter
3. Paste the URL into the embed dialog and click Embed link
4. Resize the block to fit your dashboard layout

No browser extensions, no account required for the free widgets, no app installs. The widget runs live inside Notion.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are there free aesthetic Notion widgets for students?

Yes. Blocs offers a free Pomodoro Timer, Habit Tracker, and Water Tracker with no sign-up required. You get the full widget functionality at default settings. Customization and additional widgets require the one-time Pro upgrade ($17).

### What makes a Notion widget "aesthetic"?

Aesthetic widgets are visually minimal, match or complement Notion's clean interface, and don't introduce visual clutter. Key traits: consistent typography, muted or customizable color palettes, compact layouts, and smooth interactions. Blocs widgets support theme customization in Pro so they can match your exact Notion color scheme.

### Do Notion widgets work on mobile?

Notion's mobile app has limited embed support — most embedded iframes either don't render or require tapping through to a browser view. Widgets work best on desktop (browser or desktop app). Blocs Pro includes cloud sync, so your data (habits, water goals, streaks) carries over across devices even if the widget itself is best used on desktop.

### Will embedding widgets slow down my Notion page?

Each embed adds a small iframe load. In practice, one or two widgets per page has no noticeable impact on load time. Stacking many embeds on a single page can slow things down, so a focused student dashboard with three to five widgets tends to work better than a widget-heavy mega-page.

### Do I need to know how to code to add Notion widgets?

No coding required. You copy a URL, use Notion's built-in /embed command, and paste. The entire setup for any Blocs widget takes under a minute.

### Is there a calendar widget for Notion?

Yes. Blocs offers a [Calendar widget](https://blocs.me/calendar-widget) as part of the Pro plan. It displays a visual monthly calendar with date markers, embedded directly in your Notion page.
