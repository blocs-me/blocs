## What Makes a Good Widget for a Notion Planner?

Most Notion planners fail not because of bad structure, but because key information sits in separate apps. Your water intake is in a health app. Your focus timer is a browser tab. Your goal progress is a mental note. The whole point of embedding widgets is to collapse that context-switching into one workspace.

A good Notion planner widget should:

- Embed natively as an iframe (no external login wall every session)
- Persist data across page reloads (not reset every time you open Notion)
- Be visually clean enough to live alongside your notes and databases
- Do one thing well rather than replicate Notion features you already have

Blocs was built specifically around this constraint. Every widget is designed for the Notion canvas: compact, interactive, and purpose-built for a single planning function.
