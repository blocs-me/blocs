## How Do You Embed a Widget in Notion?

Embedding a Blocs widget in Notion takes under a minute. You don't need any coding knowledge, browser extensions, or a Notion account with special permissions.

1. Copy the embed URL for the widget you want (e.g., `https://blocs.me/pomodoro`).
2. Open the Notion page where you want the widget to appear.
3. Type `/embed` in Notion to open the embed block dialog.
4. Paste the widget URL and press **Embed link**.
5. Resize the embed block to fit your layout by dragging the corners.

That's it. The widget is now live inside your Notion page and persists across sessions. For more detail, see the [step-by-step Pomodoro embed guide](https://blocs.me/blog/notion-pomodoro-timer-widget) or the [habit tracker embed walkthrough](https://blocs.me/blog/notion-habit-tracker-widget).
