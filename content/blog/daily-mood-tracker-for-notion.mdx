## Why Track Your Mood in Notion?

Most people who use Notion as their productivity hub already log their day there — tasks, journaling, goals, meeting notes. Adding a mood tracker to a separate app means context-switching at exactly the moment you're trying to reflect. That friction is usually why mood tracking habits fail.

Keeping everything in one workspace solves the consistency problem. When your mood check-in is two seconds away from your daily journal entry or your weekly review page, you actually do it. The habit sticks because it's embedded in your existing workflow, not bolted on as an afterthought.

Notion's native database tools can technically handle mood tracking, but the setup is non-trivial: you need custom properties, linked views, rollup formulas, and chart blocks to get anything resembling a visual log. That's overkill for most people who just want to tap a mood level and move on.
