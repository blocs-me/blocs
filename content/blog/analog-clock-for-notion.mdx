## How to Embed an Analog Clock in Notion

### Step 1: Get the embed URL

Navigate to [blocs.me/clock-widget](https://blocs.me/clock-widget). If you have a Blocs Pro account, sign in to access your customized clock URL with your preferred settings. If you're exploring first, you can preview the widget directly on the page.

### Step 2: Copy the widget URL

The embed path for the clock widget is `https://blocs.me/clock`. Copy this URL — you'll paste it directly into Notion.

### Step 3: Add an embed block in Notion

Open your Notion page. Type `/embed` and select the **Embed** block option. Paste the Blocs clock URL into the embed dialog and press Enter.

### Step 4: Resize and position

Notion will render the clock inline on your page. Drag the resize handle to adjust the width. For an analog clock, a compact square or slightly wide rectangle works well — around 300-400px wide gives a clean look without taking up too much space.

### Step 5: Customize (Pro)

With Blocs Pro, you can configure the display mode (analog, digital, or flip), choose a color theme, and toggle the seconds hand. Changes sync to the embed automatically.

The [digital clock mode](https://blocs.me/blog/digital-clock-for-notion) is better if you're tracking exact meeting times or running tight schedules. The [flip clock](https://blocs.me/blog/notion-clock-widget) is popular on aesthetic dashboards where visual personality matters as much as function.
