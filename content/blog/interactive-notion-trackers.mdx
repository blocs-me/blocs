## What Are Interactive Notion Trackers?

Most "Notion trackers" you find online are just database templates — rows, checkboxes, and formulas that you manage manually. They look fine in screenshots but require ongoing maintenance, break when you adjust properties, and give you no visual feedback at a glance.

Interactive Notion trackers are different. They're live widgets embedded directly into your Notion page via an iframe. You see a real visual element — a filling water glass, a habit checkmark grid, a live countdown — that updates in real time without you touching a database. The tracker does the work. You just use it.

Blocs builds exactly this kind of widget. Every tracker is designed to live *inside* your Notion workspace, not replace it.

## Why Static Notion Templates Don't Work as Trackers

Templates built with Notion databases are popular, but they have real limitations as daily trackers:

- **Manual upkeep:** You have to open the database, create a new entry, and fill in properties. Every single day.
- **No visual feedback:** A table of checkboxes doesn't tell you at a glance how you're doing. You have to query or filter to see progress.
- **Formula fragility:** Streak and summary formulas break when you add or rename properties.
- **No real-time behavior:** A database can't run a Pomodoro countdown or animate a water level filling up.

Embedded widgets solve all of these. They're interactive, self-contained, and don't require database management. You interact with the tracker directly on your dashboard page, and the data persists automatically. For more on how this works in practice, see the guide on [tracking progress in Notion](https://blocs.me/blog/how-to-track-progress-in-notion).
