## How Do You Embed a Widget in Notion?

To embed a Blocs widget in Notion, open your Notion page, type `/embed`, select the Embed block, and paste the widget URL (e.g., `https://blocs.me/habit-tracker`). Click "Embed link" and the live widget appears inline. Resize it by dragging the edges. No sign-up is required for the free widgets.

### Step-by-step: Building a Personal Growth Dashboard

1. **Create a new Notion page** — title it "Personal Growth Dashboard" or similar
2. **Add a Habit Tracker** — type `/embed`, paste `https://blocs.me/habit-tracker`, hit Enter. Set your habits (e.g., read 20 min, meditate, exercise)
3. **Add a Water Tracker** — repeat the embed step with `https://blocs.me/water-tracker`. Set your daily hydration goal

7. **Arrange the layout** — use Notion's column layout to place widgets side-by-side for a clean dashboard view

## How Does the Habit Tracker Work for Personal Growth?

The Blocs habit tracker lets you define daily habits, check them off each day, and automatically builds streaks over time. The free tier supports a set of default habits. With Pro, you get unlimited custom habits, daily and weekly analytics, streak history, and theme customization. This turns a simple Notion page into a real consistency dashboard.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do Blocs widgets save data between Notion sessions?

Free widgets save data locally in your browser. Pro widgets include cloud sync, which means your habit streaks, water intake history, and progress data persist across devices and sessions. If you clear your browser cache on the free tier, local data resets.

### Can I use these widgets on Notion mobile?

Notion's mobile app has limited embed support. The widgets work best on Notion desktop (Mac/Windows) and Notion in a desktop browser. On mobile, some embeds may display as links rather than interactive widgets depending on your Notion plan and device.

### Is the $17 Pro plan a subscription?

No. Blocs Pro is a one-time payment of $17 for lifetime access to all current and future Pro widgets. There is no monthly or annual fee. This is a key differentiator from other Notion widget services that charge recurring subscriptions.

### How many habits can I track on the free tier?

The free habit tracker supports default habits with basic settings. For unlimited custom habits and advanced analytics (streaks, weekly/monthly views), you need Blocs Pro. Most users starting a personal growth routine find the free tier sufficient for the first few weeks.

### Can I embed all widgets on the same Notion page?

Yes. You can embed as many Blocs widgets as you want on a single Notion page. Use Notion's column layout to arrange them side-by-side. A typical personal growth dashboard might have the habit tracker and water tracker in one row and the pomodoro timer and progress bar in another.

### What's the difference between a progress bar and a habit tracker for goal tracking?

A habit tracker measures daily consistency — whether you showed up. A progress bar tracks cumulative output toward a numeric target (books read, miles run, sessions completed). Both are useful: habits build the system, progress bars measure the results of that system.
