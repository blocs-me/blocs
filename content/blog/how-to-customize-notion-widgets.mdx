## What Does "Customizing a Notion Widget" Actually Mean?

Notion doesn't have a built-in widget store or visual customization panel. When people talk about customizing Notion widgets, they mean one of two things:

1. **Changing the appearance or behavior of an embeddable widget** (colors, themes, goals, units) before pasting it into Notion.
2. **Styling Notion database templates** to mimic widget-like layouts using properties and views.

This guide focuses on the first approach, which is far more powerful and far less work. Embeddable widgets like those from Blocs are configured on the provider's site and dropped into any Notion page as an iframe embed block. The widget runs live inside your workspace, with no external app to switch to.

## How to Customize and Embed a Notion Widget (Step by Step)

### Step 1: Choose Your Widget

Head to [blocs.me](https://blocs.me) and pick the widget you want. Free widgets include the [Pomodoro Timer](https://blocs.me/pomodoro-timer), [Habit Tracker](https://blocs.me/habit-tracker-widget), and [Water Tracker](https://blocs.me/water-tracker-widget). No account needed for the free tier.

### Step 2: Configure Appearance and Settings

Each widget has a settings panel where you can adjust:

- **Theme and color:** Light, dark, or a custom accent color (Pro).
- **Goals and durations:** Set daily water intake targets, work/break intervals for the Pomodoro timer, or habit goals (Pro).
- **Units:** Switch between ml/oz for water tracking, or minutes for timers.
- **Branding:** Remove the Blocs logo for a cleaner look (Pro).

On the free plan, widgets come with well-chosen defaults that work without any configuration. Pro unlocks the full range of settings above.

### Step 3: Copy the Embed URL

Once you've configured your widget, copy the embed URL provided on the widget page (for example, `https://blocs.me/pomodoro` for the Pomodoro Timer).

### Step 4: Embed in Notion

1. Open your Notion page and type `/embed` in a new block.
2. Select **Embed** from the menu.
3. Paste the widget URL and click **Embed link**.
4. Resize the block by dragging the bottom edge until the widget fits your layout.

That's it. The widget is now live inside your Notion workspace and updates in real time.

## Tips for a Better-Looking Notion Dashboard with Custom Widgets

- **Match your Notion theme first.** Decide light or dark before choosing widget themes. A dark Notion page with a light widget looks unfinished.
- **Use column blocks.** Place two or three compact widgets side by side using Notion's column layout (drag a block next to another to create columns).
- **Resize deliberately.** Drag widget blocks to roughly match the widget's natural proportions. Stretching a small widget too wide creates awkward whitespace.
- **Group by function.** Cluster focus widgets (Pomodoro, Progress Bar) in one section and health widgets (Water Tracker, Habit Tracker) in another. It makes the dashboard easier to scan.
- **Use the Progress Bar for project tracking.** Set a custom range (say, 0 to 50 tasks) and update it manually as a lightweight milestone indicator inside a project page.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can I customize a Notion widget without a Blocs account?

Yes. Free widgets (Pomodoro, Habit Tracker, Water Tracker) can be embedded with default settings and no account required. A Blocs account is only needed to save custom settings and access Pro features.

### Do widget customizations save automatically?

With a Blocs Pro account, settings are saved to the cloud and sync across devices. On the free tier without an account, default settings are used each time the widget loads.

### Can I use multiple themes across different widgets?

Yes. Each widget is configured independently, so your Pomodoro Timer can use a dark theme while your Habit Tracker uses a light theme. This is useful if you have multiple Notion pages with different styling.

### Will resizing the Notion embed block break the widget?

No. Blocs widgets are responsive and reflow within the embed block. Resize freely; the widget adjusts to fit the available width. Use Notion's drag handle on the bottom edge of the embed block to set the height.

### Can I embed the same widget on multiple Notion pages?

Yes. Copy the embed URL and paste it into as many Notion pages as you like. With a Pro account and cloud sync, data (habits completed, water logged) is consistent across all instances.

### What if Blocs doesn't offer a widget I need?

The Blocs team is actively expanding the widget library. Check [blocs.me/faqs](https://blocs.me/faqs) or reach out at support@blocs.me to ask about upcoming widgets. Several new widget types are in active development.
