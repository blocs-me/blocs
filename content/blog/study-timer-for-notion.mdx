## Best Practices for Studying with a Pomodoro Timer in Notion

- **Create a dedicated study template.** Build a Notion page template with your timer embed at the top, a task checklist below it, and a notes section underneath. Duplicate it for each study session.
- **Use a two-column layout.** Put the timer on the right, your active notes on the left. This keeps the countdown visible without dominating the page.
- **Log what you worked on each Pomodoro.** After each 25-minute block, write one sentence about what you accomplished in a running log below the timer.
- **Set a session goal before you start.** Write "Today I'll finish: ___" at the top of the page before starting the timer. The visible goal increases follow-through.
- **Take breaks seriously.** The 5-minute break is not optional — it's where the productivity gains come from. Step away from your desk, don't scroll.
