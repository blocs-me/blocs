This works for one car or a whole household fleet.

## What do you need to track for car maintenance in Notion?

You need five data points at minimum: the service performed, the date it was done, the odometer reading, the cost, and the next due date or mileage. These five fields let you build every view you'll actually use: upcoming services, cost history, and per-vehicle logs.

If you own multiple vehicles, add a "Vehicle" relation or select property so you can filter the same database by car instead of duplicating pages. Optional but useful fields: shop/mechanic name, warranty status, and a file upload property for receipts.

## How do you set up a car maintenance tracker in Notion?

Create a new database, add the core properties listed above, then build three views: a Table view sorted by Next Due Date, a Calendar view for visual scheduling, and a Gallery view grouped by vehicle. This takes about 10-15 minutes for a single car.

### 1. Create the database

Start a new page, insert a full-page database, and name it "Car Maintenance Log." Add properties: Service (select: Oil Change, Tire Rotation, Brake Pads, Inspection, etc.), Date (date), Mileage (number), Cost (number, format as currency), Next Due Date (date), and Notes (text).

### 2. Build a "due soon" view

Add a Table view, filter where Next Due Date is within the next 30 days, and sort ascending. This becomes your at-a-glance "what needs attention" list without scrolling through your full history.

### 3. Add a visual reminder widget

Databases are great for records but easy to forget because they're not visually loud. Embedding a widget directly on your dashboard page solves that, since it's the first thing you see when you open Notion.

A [habit tracker widget](https://blocs.me/habit-tracker-widget) works well for recurring, frequency-based maintenance (checking tire pressure monthly, washing/waxing, fluid top-offs) because it gives you a visual streak and daily/weekly grid. For a specific hard deadline (like "next oil change in 18 days" or "registration renewal in 42 days"), a [countdown timer](https://blocs.me/countdown-timer) is a better fit since it counts down to one fixed date.

## How often should I actually service my car?

Intervals vary by manufacturer and engine type, but general guidance is: oil changes every 5,000-7,500 miles (3,000 for older or high-mileage engines), tire rotations every 5,000-8,000 miles, brake inspections every 10,000-12,000 miles, and cabin air filters every 12,000-15,000 miles. Always check your specific owner's manual for exact numbers, since these ranges differ by make and model.

## FAQs

### Can I track multiple cars in one Notion database?

Yes. Add a Vehicle select or relation property to your maintenance database, then filter or group each view by vehicle. This keeps one shared log instead of duplicating databases per car.

### Does Notion send automatic reminders for due maintenance?

Notion itself doesn't send push notifications for database date fields unless you set up a linked reminder or use the Notion mobile app's date-based notifications, which are limited. A visual widget like a [countdown timer](https://blocs.me/countdown-timer) solves this by making the due date impossible to miss on your dashboard.
