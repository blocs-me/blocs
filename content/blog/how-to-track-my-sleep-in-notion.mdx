## What is the best way to track sleep in Notion?

The best setup pairs a Notion database (for logging exact sleep data) with a visual habit widget (for tracking consistency over time). The database captures numbers; the widget makes the pattern obvious without opening a table every morning.

### Step 1: Build a sleep log database

Create a new database with these properties:

- **Date** (date property)
- **Bedtime** and **Wake time** (text or date-with-time properties)
- **Duration** (formula property calculating the difference between wake time and bedtime)
- **Quality** (select: Poor / Fair / Good / Great, or a 1-5 number rating)
- **Notes** (text, for caffeine, screen time, stress, etc.)

Add a formula like `dateBetween(prop("Wake time"), prop("Bedtime"), "hours")` to auto-calculate duration so you're not doing mental math at 7am.

### Step 2: Add a visual habit widget for consistency

A database table is great for detail but bad for a quick glance. Embed a habit-tracker widget above or beside your log so you can see your streak of "good sleep nights" without opening the full database.

## How do I track sleep duration and quality in Notion?

Track duration with a formula property that subtracts bedtime from wake time, and track quality with a simple select or number rating filled in each morning. Add a rollup or chart view (via a linked database or third-party chart embed) to visualize weekly averages.

For a lighter-weight option, skip the formula and just log hours slept as a number (e.g., 7.5). It's less precise but faster to fill in daily, which matters more for consistency than precision, per general sleep-tracking guidance from the [Sleep Foundation](https://www.sleepfoundation.org/sleep-hygiene/sleep-tracking).

## FAQs

### Does Blocs have a dedicated sleep tracker widget?

Not yet. Blocs currently offers Pomodoro, Water, and Habit Tracker widgets for free, plus Countdown, Progress Bar, Clock, Calendar, Quote, and Weather widgets with Pro. A dedicated sleep widget is on the roadmap; in the meantime, the Habit Tracker widget works well for tracking sleep consistency as a daily habit.

### Can I track exact sleep hours with Blocs?

The Habit Tracker widget is designed for yes/no daily habits (like "slept 7+ hours"), not minute-by-minute duration logging. For exact hours, use a Notion database with a duration formula, and use the widget alongside it for the visual streak.

### How do I calculate sleep duration automatically in Notion?

Add a formula property that subtracts your Bedtime date-time property from your Wake time date-time property, converted to hours (e.g., `dateBetween(prop("Wake time"), prop("Bedtime"), "hours")`). This updates automatically every time you log new times.

### Is tracking sleep in Notion free?

Yes, building a sleep database in Notion is free, and embedding the Blocs Habit Tracker widget's default version is also free with no sign-up required. Pro features (unlimited habits, analytics, themes, no branding) cost $17 one-time.

### Can I sync sleep data from a smartwatch or phone app into Notion?

Not directly through Blocs. Some users use third-party automation tools (like Zapier or native app exports) to push wearable sleep data into a Notion database, which you can then pair with a Blocs widget for the visual layer. This requires separate integration tools outside of Blocs.

### What's a simpler alternative to a full sleep database?

If a full database with formulas feels like too much upkeep, just embed the [Habit Tracker widget](https://blocs.me/habit-tracker-widget) and track one binary sleep habit (like "in bed by 11pm"). It takes seconds to check off daily and still gives you a visual streak.
