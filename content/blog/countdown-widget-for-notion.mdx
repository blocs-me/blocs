## What Is a Countdown Widget for Notion?

A countdown widget for Notion is an interactive timer embedded directly inside a Notion page. Because Notion supports iframe embeds, you can drop in a live countdown that ticks down to any target date or time — a product launch, an exam, a deadline, or a personal milestone.

Unlike a static date property in a Notion database, a countdown widget is visual and dynamic. It shows days, hours, minutes, and seconds in real time, giving you and your team an immediate sense of urgency without leaving the workspace.

## Why Notion Doesn't Have a Built-In Countdown

Notion's native date fields let you record due dates, but they don't display a ticking countdown. There's no built-in way to show "47 hours and 22 minutes until launch" on a Notion page. Workarounds — like using formulas to calculate days remaining — require manual setup, are hard to maintain, and look nothing like a real timer.

The practical solution is an embed: a small, purpose-built widget hosted externally that Notion renders inline. This is exactly what [Blocs' countdown timer](https://blocs.me/countdown-timer) is designed for.

## How to Add a Countdown Widget to Notion

### Step 1: Get your embed URL

Go to [blocs.me/countdown-timer](https://blocs.me/countdown-timer). Sign in (or sign up) to access the Pro countdown widget. Set your target date and time, then copy the embed URL provided.

### Step 2: Open Notion and create an embed block

In your Notion page, type `/embed` and select the Embed block. Paste the Blocs countdown URL into the embed field and press Enter.

### Step 3: Resize and position

Drag the embed block to the size you want. The countdown widget is responsive and looks clean at most widths. You can place it at the top of a project dashboard, next to a task list, or in a dedicated "Goals" page.

### Step 4: Customize (Pro)

With Blocs Pro, you can adjust the label text, choose themes, and set custom date ranges. The widget syncs across devices via cloud, so your team sees the same live countdown whether they're on desktop or mobile.
