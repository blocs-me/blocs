There isn't a dedicated drag-and-drop grocery list widget you can embed in Notion today. Most "notion grocery list" results you'll find online are actually page templates (duplicate-and-edit Notion pages), not embeddable widgets like a Pomodoro timer or calendar. That distinction matters if you specifically want something that updates live via iframe, syncs across devices automatically, or sits inside a dashboard alongside other widgets.

## How do you add a grocery list to Notion?

Type a line of text, then press `Ctrl+/` (or `Cmd+/` on Mac) and search "to-do list," or use the markdown shortcut: type `[]` followed by a space. Notion instantly converts it into a checkbox you can tick off as you shop ([Notion Help Center](https://www.notion.so/help/writing-and-editing-basics)).

### Option 1: Native checklist (fastest)

Add a new page, type your items as checkboxes, and check them off on your phone at the store. No setup, but no categories, quantities, or recurring reset.

### Option 2: Database with properties

Create a database and add properties like "Category" (produce, dairy, pantry), "Quantity," and a checkbox for "In Cart." This is more flexible but takes longer to set up, and you're rebuilding it from a template each week unless you add a recurring-reset automation ([Notion database docs](https://www.notion.so/help/intro-to-databases)).

### Option 3: Pair it with a habit widget

If your real problem is remembering to go grocery shopping (not just what to buy), a checklist alone won't fix that. Embedding a habit-tracking widget next to your list turns "grocery run" into something you track weekly, with streaks and reminders.

## FAQs

### Does Notion have a built-in grocery list template?

Notion doesn't ship a default grocery template, but its template gallery includes community-made grocery and meal-planning databases you can duplicate for free ([Notion database docs](https://www.notion.so/help/intro-to-databases)).

### Can I check off grocery items from my phone while shopping?

Yes. Any checklist or database you build in Notion syncs to the mobile app in real time, so items checked off in-store update instantly on desktop.

### Is there any way to embed a shopping list as a widget in Notion?
