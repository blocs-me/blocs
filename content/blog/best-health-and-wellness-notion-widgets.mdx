## Why add wellness widgets to a Notion workspace?

Most people already use Notion for planning, so keeping health tracking in the same place removes an extra app from the daily routine. Instead of building a water-intake formula from scratch or downloading a separate habit-tracking app, you paste one link and get an interactive widget that logs itself.

This matters because habit tracking tends to fail when logging takes more than a few seconds. A dense database table with formulas and rollups is harder to update on a phone than tapping a glass icon on a Water Tracker widget. Dedicated wellness widgets are designed around quick daily taps, not spreadsheet entry.

## How do I add a wellness widget to Notion?

Copy a widget's embed URL (e.g., blocs.me/water-tracker), paste it into a Notion page, and select "Embed" from the link menu. The widget renders live and saves your data automatically - no code, database setup, or template import needed.

1. Open the widget page you want (Water Tracker, Habit Tracker, or Pomodoro Timer) and copy its URL.
2. In Notion, type `/embed` or paste the URL directly on its own line.
3. Choose "Embed link" when Notion prompts you.
4. Resize the embed block to fit your dashboard layout.
5. Optional: sign in to Blocs to sync Pro settings and unlock the full widget library.
