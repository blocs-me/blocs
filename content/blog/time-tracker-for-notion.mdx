## How to Embed a Time Tracker in Notion?

To embed a Blocs time tracker in Notion, type `/embed` on any Notion page, paste the widget URL (e.g., `https://blocs.me/pomodoro`), and press Enter. Resize the embed block to fit your layout. The widget is live immediately, no account needed for the free tier.

Step-by-step:

1. Open any Notion page where you want the timer.
2. Type `/embed` and select the Embed block from the menu.
3. Paste `https://blocs.me/pomodoro` (or your chosen widget URL) into the input field.
4. Click **Embed link**. The timer appears inline.
5. Drag the bottom edge of the embed block to adjust the height.

For a detailed walkthrough with tips on sizing and placement, see the guide on [how to embed a Pomodoro timer in Notion](https://blocs.me/blog/how-to-embed-pomodoro-in-notion).

Manual Notion databases are another common workaround: logging start and end times in a table. This works for historical records but offers no running timer or real-time countdown. Blocs widgets handle the live, in-the-moment tracking piece that databases can't.
