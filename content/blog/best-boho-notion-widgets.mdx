## What makes a notion widget "boho" aesthetic?

A boho Notion widget uses warm neutral palettes (terracotta, sand, sage, cream) instead of default blues and grays, paired with minimal borders and soft rounded shapes. Most Notion widgets can't do this because their colors are hardcoded. [Blocs](https://blocs.me) widgets solve this with built-in theme controls so you pick the palette instead of inheriting one.

## How do I add a boho widget to Notion?

Pick a Blocs widget, copy its embed URL, then paste it into an `/embed` block on your Notion page. Resize the block to fit your layout, and if you're on Pro, open the widget's settings link to switch its color theme to a boho palette before saving.

### Step-by-step

- Go to the widget's page (e.g. [Habit Tracker](https://blocs.me/habit-tracker-widget)) and copy the embed link
- In Notion, type `/embed` and paste the link
- Resize the block so it fits your page's grid
- If using Pro, click the widget's settings gear and choose a warm/neutral theme
- Repeat for each widget you want on the page

## FAQs

### Is there a dedicated "boho" theme in Blocs?

Blocs doesn't ship a single preset labeled "boho." Instead, Pro gives you a color picker so you can set each widget's palette to warm neutrals (terracotta, sand, sage) yourself, which covers the boho look without a locked-in template.

### Can I get the boho look for free?

Partially. The free Pomodoro, Water Tracker, and Habit Tracker widgets have clean, minimal default designs that read as boho-adjacent, but full color customization requires [Blocs Pro](https://blocs.me/pricing) ($17 one-time).

### Do boho Notion widgets slow down my page?

No. Blocs widgets are lightweight iframes, not plugins or scripts installed into Notion, so they load like any embedded web page and don't affect Notion's native performance.

### What's the difference between a boho widget and a boho template graphic?

A template graphic (an image someone designed to look like a tracker) can't actually track anything, you'd edit it manually. A Blocs widget is a live, interactive tool, such as the [Water Tracker](https://blocs.me/water-tracker-widget), that updates in real time while still matching your color scheme.

### Do I need to install anything outside Notion?

No. Every Blocs widget works through Notion's built-in `/embed` block, officially documented on [Notion's site](https://www.notion.so). There's no browser extension or separate app to install.

### Is Blocs Pro a subscription?

No. Blocs Pro is a one-time $17 payment for lifetime access to all nine widgets, theme customization, and analytics, not a recurring subscription. Details are on the [pricing page](https://blocs.me/pricing).
