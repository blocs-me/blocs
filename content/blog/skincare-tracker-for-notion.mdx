## How do you track a skincare routine in Notion?

You track a skincare routine in Notion by embedding a habit-tracking widget on your page and renaming the default habits to your routine steps, or by building a manual database with checkbox properties for each day. The widget route takes under a minute and requires no formulas; a manual database takes longer but allows more custom fields.

### Option 1: Embed a widget (fastest)

1. Go to [blocs.me/habit-tracker-widget](https://blocs.me/habit-tracker-widget) and copy the embed URL.
2. In Notion, type `/embed` and paste the URL.
3. Rename the default habit labels to your AM/PM skincare steps.
4. Check off steps daily; the widget tracks your streak automatically.

### Option 2: Build a manual database

1. Create a new database with a Date property and checkbox columns for each step.
2. Add a rollup or formula property to calculate weekly completion rate.
3. Duplicate the template weekly or set it to repeat.

For a side-by-side walkthrough of both approaches, see our dedicated guide on [how to log your skincare routine in Notion](https://blocs.me/blog/how-to-log-skincare-routine-in-notion).
