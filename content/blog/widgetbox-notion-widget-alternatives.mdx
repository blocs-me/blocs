## What is WidgetBox and why look for alternatives?

[WidgetBox](https://widgetbox.app) is a widget-embedding platform that lets users add visual elements like clocks, counters, and decorative embeds to Notion pages, websites, and other platforms via iframe. People look for alternatives when they want widgets built specifically for Notion workflows (habit tracking, pomodoro sessions, water intake) with built-in analytics, rather than general-purpose decorative embeds, or when they want to avoid branding and subscription pricing on premium features.

## How do you add a widget to Notion?

In Notion, type `/embed`, paste the widget's URL (for example, blocs.me/habit-tracker), and press enter. The widget then renders live inside your Notion page as an interactive iframe, not a static screenshot, so timers, counters, and trackers update in real time without leaving your workspace.
