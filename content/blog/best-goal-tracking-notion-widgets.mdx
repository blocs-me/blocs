## What Are Goal Tracking Notion Widgets?

Goal tracking Notion widgets are embeddable mini-apps that display inside a Notion page via an iframe embed block. Instead of building manual databases or relying on static checkboxes, these widgets give you live, interactive trackers — habit streaks, progress percentages, countdown timers — that update in real time directly inside your workspace.

Notion's native tools (checkboxes, progress formulas, date properties) require significant setup and don't offer visual feedback like rings, bars, or animated countdowns. Widgets fill that gap without requiring you to leave Notion or manage a separate app.

## How Do You Embed Goal Tracking Widgets in Notion?

Embedding a Blocs widget in Notion takes under two minutes and requires no account for free widgets.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Do Notion goal tracking widgets save my data?

Free Blocs widgets store data locally in your browser. Pro widgets with cloud sync enabled save your data to your Blocs account, so your habit streaks and progress persist across devices and browser sessions.

### Is there a completely free goal tracker for Notion?

Yes. The Blocs Habit Tracker and Water Tracker are free with no account required. They cover daily habit goals and hydration targets out of the box. For progress bars and countdown timers, a one-time $17 Pro upgrade is required.

### Can I add multiple goal tracking widgets to one Notion page?

Yes. You can embed as many Blocs widgets as you want on a single Notion page. A common setup is a "Goals" dashboard with a Habit Tracker, a Progress Bar for a specific target, and a Countdown Timer to a deadline, all on the same page.

### Should I use Notion formulas or a widget for goal tracking?

Notion formulas work for simple calculated progress (e.g., tasks completed / total tasks), but they require manual maintenance, break when database structures change, and don't offer visual animations or streak logic. Widgets handle the display and logic for you and are faster to set up for most goal tracking use cases.
