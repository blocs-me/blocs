## What other options exist for tracking fasting in Notion?

Most people currently track fasting in Notion through manual databases (a table with date, start time, end time, and a formula to calculate hours), which works but requires maintenance and formula debugging every time you tweak your schedule. Others use separate fasting apps on their phone and then manually copy results into Notion, which breaks the "everything in one place" workflow. An embedded habit-tracker widget avoids both problems: no formulas to build, and no app-switching, since the check-in happens right where you already plan your day.
