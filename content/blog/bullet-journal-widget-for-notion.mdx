## Why build a bullet journal in Notion instead of paper?

A digital bullet journal in Notion syncs across devices, backs itself up automatically, and lets you embed live trackers instead of redrawing grids every month. The trade-off is that you lose the tactile, slow-down ritual that draws many people to analog bullet journaling in the first place.

Notion's own database and gallery views can mimic a habit log, but they require manual property setup, rollups, and formulas to get a visual tracker that looks decent. Widget-based tools skip that setup entirely: you paste an embed link and the tracker is already styled and functional.

## How do I set up a bullet journal with Notion widgets?

Create a Notion page for your journal, then embed each widget using Notion's "/embed" block with the widget's URL. Arrange habit, water, and pomodoro embeds side by side to recreate a weekly spread in minutes, no coding or database setup required.

### Step-by-step

- Open the page in Notion where you want your bullet journal spread.
- Type `/embed` and paste the widget URL (e.g., `blocs.me/habit-tracker`).
- Resize the embed block to fit your layout, then repeat for each tracker page.
- Upgrade to [Blocs Pro](https://blocs.me/pricing) if you want the calendar or quote widgets for your monthly/daily log pages.
