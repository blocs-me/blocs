## How to Add a Pink Widget to Notion

Embedding a Blocs widget in Notion takes under a minute:

1. Go to [blocs.me](https://blocs.me) and open the widget you want (e.g., `blocs.me/pomodoro`).
2. Copy the widget URL from your browser's address bar.
3. In Notion, type `/embed` and select the Embed block.
4. Paste the URL and press Enter. The widget loads inline.
5. To enable a pink theme, sign in and upgrade to Pro ($17 one-time). Go to widget settings and select your preferred color palette.

No sign-up is required for the free tier. The free widgets (pomodoro, habit tracker, water tracker) work immediately on embed.

## Tips for Building a Pink Notion Dashboard

- **Start with a pink Notion cover and icon.** Notion's native cover and icon settings let you set the page header. Use a soft pink gradient image as your cover before adding any widgets.
- **Use callout blocks with pink emoji.** Notion's callout blocks support emoji. A pink heart or flower emoji beside a callout creates visual anchors on the page.
- **Arrange widgets in columns.** Use Notion's column layout (drag a block beside another) to place a timer next to a habit tracker. Blocs widgets scale cleanly in columns.
- **Match your widget theme to your page accent color.** Blocs Pro lets you set custom widget colors. Aim for a consistent hex value across all your widgets to avoid visual clutter.
- **Use the Quote of the Day widget as a header element.** Placed at the top of a dashboard, it adds an aesthetic greeting without any maintenance required.
