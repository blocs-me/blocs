## How do I track calories in Notion?

Create a database with properties for Food, Calories, Protein, Carbs, Fat, and Date, then log each meal as a new entry. Add a linked "Daily Totals" view that groups entries by date and uses a Sum rollup to show how many calories you've eaten that day versus your target.

### Step 1: Create a food log database

Add a new full-page database called "Food Log." Give it properties for Food Name (Title), Calories (Number), Protein/Carbs/Fat (Number), Meal (Select: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snack), and Date (Date).

### Step 2: Build a daily totals rollup

Create a second database called "Daily Log" with one entry per date. Add a Relation property linking to Food Log entries from that day, then add a Rollup property that sums the Calories field. This gives you a running daily total without manual math.

### Step 3: Set up calendar and board views

Switch your Food Log to a Calendar view to see meals by day, and add a Board view grouped by Meal to spot patterns (like consistently high-calorie dinners). Notion's own [template gallery](https://www.notion.so/templates) has free nutrition and meal-planning templates you can duplicate as a starting point rather than building from scratch.

## Does Notion have a built-in calorie tracker?

No. Notion is a database and notes tool, not a nutrition app, so it has no native calorie counter, food database, or barcode scanner. Everything from macro columns to daily totals has to be built manually with properties, formulas, or rollups, which is flexible but takes setup time compared to a dedicated app.

## FAQs

### Can I scan barcodes to log calories in Notion?

No, Notion has no barcode scanning or food database, so every entry (including calorie and macro values) has to be typed in manually or copied from a nutrition label or app like MyFitnessPal.

### Will my calorie totals update automatically each day?

Yes, if you use a Rollup property linked from your Daily Log to your Food Log entries, Notion recalculates the sum automatically every time you add or edit a meal entry for that date.

### Is the Blocs Habit Tracker a calorie counter?

No, it's a visual daily check-in and streak tool, not a nutrition database. It's best used to track whether you hit a goal (like staying under a calorie target) that you calculate separately in a Notion database.

### Do I need Blocs Pro to track calories in Notion?

No, the free Blocs Habit Tracker covers one daily goal check-in at no cost. Pro ($17 one-time) is only needed if you want unlimited habits, custom goal ranges, and streak analytics across weeks and months.

### What's the easiest Notion calorie template to start with?

A single database with Food, Calories, Macros, Meal, and Date properties is the simplest setup. You can duplicate a starter template from [Notion's own template gallery](https://www.notion.so/templates) instead of building every property from scratch.

### How many calories should my daily goal be?

It depends on age, sex, height, and activity level, the [CDC](https://www.cdc.gov/healthyweight/calorie_counting/index.html) estimates most adults need between 1,600 and 3,000 calories per day. Set your Notion goal property to match a target from a doctor, dietitian, or a calculator based on these guidelines.
