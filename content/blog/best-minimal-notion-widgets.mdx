## What Are Minimal Notion Widgets?

Minimal Notion widgets are small, embeddable tools you add to a Notion page via iframe embed. They handle specific productivity tasks — tracking habits, timing work sessions, logging water intake — without the visual weight of full dashboards or the friction of switching to a separate app. "Minimal" means clean design: limited color, clear hierarchy, and no distracting animations or ads.

Most Notion users discover widgets when they want their workspace to look and feel more like a focused productivity tool. The challenge is finding widgets that actually match Notion's clean aesthetic instead of clashing with it. The options break down into three types:

- **Standalone embedded tools** — iframes that live inside a Notion page (like Blocs)
- **Notion-native databases** — built with Notion's own blocks, but limited in interactivity
- **External apps with Notion integrations** — require leaving Notion or installing browser extensions

For a truly minimal setup, embedded iframes win. They require no extra accounts, no extensions, and no tab switching.

## How Do You Embed Blocs Widgets in Notion?

Embedding a Blocs widget in Notion takes under a minute. Copy the widget URL, paste it into a Notion embed block, and resize to fit your layout. No sign-up required for free widgets.

1. Open your Notion page and type `/embed` to insert an embed block
2. Paste the widget URL (e.g., `https://blocs.me/pomodoro`) and press "Embed link"
3. Resize the block by dragging the corners — taller for the habit tracker or water tracker, more compact for the clock or progress bar
4. For Pro widgets, sign in at [blocs.me/sign-in](https://blocs.me/sign-in) first so your settings and data sync across devices
