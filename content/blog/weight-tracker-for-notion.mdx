## Why isn't there a dedicated Notion weight tracker widget yet?

Weight tracking needs a numeric input with a trend line, not just a yes/no checkbox, which is a different data model than most Notion widgets are built around. Most Notion widget makers, including [Blocs](https://blocs.me), started with checkbox-style habit and water trackers because they're simpler to build and cover more use cases out of the box. A true weight tracker (with trend charts, goal weight, and unit toggles for lbs/kg) is a natural next step, and it's on the roadmap for Blocs.

## What's the best way to track weight in Notion right now?

Right now, the most practical option is to use a flexible tracker widget that lets you log a daily entry and view it over time, rather than building a manual Notion database from scratch. Blocs' [Habit Tracker widget](https://blocs.me/habit-tracker-widget) works well for this: set up a daily "habit" called something like "Log weight" or "Weigh-in," check it off once you've recorded the number elsewhere (like a Notion property or a note), and use the built-in streak view to stay consistent.
