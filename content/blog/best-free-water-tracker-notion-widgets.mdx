## What Is a Notion Water Tracker Widget?

A Notion water tracker widget is an interactive element embedded inside a Notion page that lets you log daily water intake with one click, see your progress toward a daily goal, and optionally review trends over time. Unlike a Notion database or table you build manually, a widget is a live web app loaded via iframe — it updates instantly and handles all the logic for you.

The alternative — building a manual hydration tracker in Notion using a database, checkboxes, or rollups — works, but it requires ongoing maintenance and offers no visual feedback. An embeddable widget gives you a purpose-built UI that behaves like an app without leaving Notion.
