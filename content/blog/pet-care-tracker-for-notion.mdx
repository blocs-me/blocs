## What is a pet care tracker for Notion?

## How do you set up a pet care tracker in Notion?

Go to [blocs.me/habit-tracker-widget](https://blocs.me/habit-tracker-widget), customize the habit names and goal count, copy the generated embed URL, then paste it into a Notion page using the `/embed` block — Notion renders it as a live, interactive widget on the page.

1. Open the [Habit Tracker widget page](https://blocs.me/habit-tracker-widget).
2. Rename the default habit slots to pet-care tasks: "Morning feed," "Evening walk," "Meds," "Litter/clean up," "Grooming."
3. Set how many times per day/week each task repeats (e.g., feeding twice daily, walks once daily).
4. Copy the embed link Blocs generates for your configuration.
5. In Notion, type `/embed`, paste the link, and resize the block to fit your page.

For a more detailed walkthrough of the embed step itself, see [how to add a habit tracker to Notion](https://blocs.me/blog/how-to-add-habit-tracker-to-notion).

## What should you actually track for a pet?

At minimum, track feeding times, medication doses, and walks or litter changes — these are the tasks most likely to be missed or duplicated between household members. Vet visits and grooming are lower-frequency but worth a monthly check-in habit so they don't slip.

| Task | Suggested frequency | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Feeding | 1-3x/day | Most commonly duplicated or missed in multi-person households |
| Medication | As prescribed | Missed doses are the highest-risk failure point |
| Walks | 1-2x/day (dogs) | Easy to track as a simple daily habit |
| Litter/cage cleaning | Daily | Hygiene and health indicator |
| Grooming | Weekly/monthly | Low frequency, easy to forget without a reminder |
| Vet visits | As scheduled | Worth a recurring monthly check-in habit |
