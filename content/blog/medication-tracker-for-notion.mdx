## How does a habit tracker work for medication tracking?

A habit tracker treats each medication dose as a recurring task you check off once taken, logging the date automatically and building a visual streak. This mirrors how adherence apps work, but lives inside your existing Notion page instead of a separate app.

Most people managing medication schedules end up in one of three setups:

- **A manual Notion database** with a date property and a checkbox, rebuilt or filtered every week.
- **A separate medication reminder app** on their phone, disconnected from their notes, symptoms, or doctor-visit prep in Notion.
- **An embedded widget** that sits on the same page as everything else, no context-switching required.

The third option is why embeddable tools have grown popular for personal health tracking in Notion, as covered in Blocs' own [guide to tracking mental health in Notion](https://blocs.me/blog/mental-health-tracker-for-notion), which uses the same underlying habit-check-in mechanic for mood and symptom logging.

### How to set it up

No. Blocs widgets currently log check-ins; they don't send push notifications or reminders. For now, pair the widget with a phone alarm or Notion reminder for the actual reminder piece.

### Is the Notion medication tracker free?

Only with Blocs Pro, which includes cloud sync. The free tier stores data locally to the embed and may not persist identically across every device without Pro.

With the free tier, one habit means one item at a time, so you'd need to swap it manually. With Pro's unlimited habits, you can list prescriptions and supplements as separate rows.
