## What is a screen time tracker for Notion?

A screen time tracker for Notion is any widget or template that helps you monitor and limit how much time you spend on devices or specific apps, displayed directly inside a Notion page. Most "trackers" in this category are actually habit-tracking tools where you manually log whether you hit a screen time goal each day, since Notion itself cannot read phone usage data.

## Does Notion have a built-in screen time tracker?

No. Notion has no native integration with iOS Screen Time, Android Digital Wellbeing, or browser extensions that track usage. Any screen time data you see in Notion has to be entered manually or imported via a third-party automation, since Notion's API doesn't expose device-level usage metrics.
