## What is a Pomodoro Widget for Notion?

A Pomodoro widget for Notion is an interactive timer that embeds directly inside a Notion page using Notion's built-in `/embed` block. Instead of opening a separate timer app, you interact with the timer right on the same page as your tasks or notes. It runs the classic Pomodoro structure: 25 minutes of focused work, a 5-minute short break, and a longer break after four cycles.

The [Pomodoro Technique](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pomodoro_Technique) was created by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s and remains one of the most widely used time management methods. Studies on structured work-break cycles consistently show reductions in decision fatigue and improved sustained concentration, which is why knowledge workers have adopted it at scale.
