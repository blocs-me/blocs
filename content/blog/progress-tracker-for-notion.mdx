## Why Does Notion Need a Progress Tracker?

Notion is a powerful workspace, but it has no built-in visual progress widget. You can build a progress bar using formulas and properties, but the result is a text-based percentage in a database cell — not a visual indicator that motivates action. A dedicated progress tracker widget gives you the glanceable, satisfying visual feedback that actually changes behavior.

Most people solve this one of three ways:

- **Manual formula bars** — Notion's native progress property works inside databases but looks plain, requires manual input, and can't be styled or made prominent on a dashboard.
- **Separate apps** — Tools like Todoist or Habitica track progress well, but they live outside Notion, breaking your workflow and splitting your attention across multiple tabs.
- **Embedded widgets** — Services like Blocs let you paste a single URL into Notion's embed block and get a fully interactive, styled progress tracker that lives right on your page.

The embed approach wins for most users because it keeps everything inside Notion without requiring you to learn database formulas or maintain a secondary tool.

## How Do You Embed a Progress Tracker in Notion?

Embedding a Blocs progress tracker in Notion takes under two minutes. Notion supports any iframe-based embed through its built-in embed block — no plugins, no Notion API access, no third-party integrations needed.

### Step 1: Get your widget URL

Go to [blocs.me/progress-bar-widget](https://blocs.me/progress-bar-widget) and sign in (or create a free account). Configure your goal: set a label (e.g., "Books read this year"), a target value (e.g., 24), and choose a color theme. Copy the embed URL provided on the widget page.

### Step 2: Open your Notion page

Navigate to the Notion page where you want the tracker. Click into an empty line and type `/embed` to open the embed block. Paste your Blocs URL and press Enter.

### Step 3: Resize and position

Notion will render the widget inline. Drag the corners to resize it. For a compact progress bar, set the height to around 150px. For a more prominent goal-tracking block, give it 200-250px of height.

### Step 4: Update your progress

Click directly inside the embedded widget to increment your progress, or type in a new value. Changes sync to the cloud automatically with Pro, so the same progress shows up whether you open Notion on desktop or mobile.

## What's the Difference Between a Progress Tracker and a Habit Tracker for Notion?

A progress tracker measures movement toward a fixed numeric goal (e.g., "read 20 books"). A habit tracker measures consistency of repeated daily behaviors (e.g., "did I exercise today?"). Both live well inside Notion as embeds, but they serve different psychological purposes: progress bars build momentum toward a finish line, while habit trackers reinforce routines through streaks and consistency data.

Blocs offers both. The Progress Bar widget suits goal-based milestones; the [Habit Tracker for Notion](https://blocs.me/blog/habit-tracker-for-notion) suits daily routines and streak-building. Many users embed both on the same Notion dashboard.
