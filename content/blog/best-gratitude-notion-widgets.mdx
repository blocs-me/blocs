## What are gratitude notion widgets?

Gratitude notion widgets are small embeddable tools that help you build and track a daily gratitude practice directly inside a Notion page, instead of switching to a separate journaling app. They typically combine a habit-streak tracker with a prompt or quote generator so you have both the "did I do it" record and the "what do I write about" nudge in one place.

- **Habit/streak tracking:** logs whether you completed your gratitude entry each day and visualizes your streak.
- **Prompt generators:** surface a daily quote or question so you're never staring at a blank Notion block.
- **Analytics:** weekly/monthly views that show how consistent you've been over time.

## Why practice gratitude in Notion instead of a separate app?

Practicing gratitude in Notion keeps the habit next to your daily notes and tasks, which lowers the friction that causes most journaling habits to lapse. If the tracker lives where you already open your planner every morning, you're more likely to actually fill it in instead of forgetting a dedicated app exists.

The behavioral case for this is well documented. A randomized study published in [Greater Good in Action](https://ggia.berkeley.edu/) (UC Berkeley's Greater Good Science Center) found that participants who wrote brief gratitude letters or journaled regularly reported significantly better mental health outcomes weeks later, even when they didn't send the letters. The habit-formation principle at play, "habit stacking" (attaching a new habit to an existing routine), is outlined by [James Clear](https://jamesclear.com/habit-stacking) and is exactly what embedding a gratitude widget in your existing Notion workspace accomplishes.
