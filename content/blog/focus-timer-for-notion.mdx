## Does the Pomodoro Technique Actually Improve Focus?

The Pomodoro Technique structures work into 25-minute intervals separated by short breaks. Research consistently links time-boxed work sessions to reduced mental fatigue and improved sustained attention. The method was developed by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s and remains one of the most widely adopted productivity frameworks among knowledge workers. Its core strength is making large, amorphous tasks feel approachable by breaking them into fixed, manageable chunks.

Where people struggle is enforcement: a timer on your phone means picking it up, which risks notifications pulling you away. A timer embedded in your Notion workspace removes that vector entirely.
