## What is the 75 Hard Challenge?

75 Hard is a 75-day mental toughness program created by entrepreneur Andy Frisella. Every day you must complete five non-negotiable tasks: two 45-minute workouts (one must be outdoors), follow a chosen diet with zero alcohol or cheat meals, drink 1 gallon (3.7 litres) of water, read 10 pages of a non-fiction or self-improvement book, and take a daily progress photo. No modifications. No missed days. If you slip on any single task, you restart from day 1 — no exceptions.

According to [Andy Frisella's official program page](https://andyfrisella.com/blogs/realaf-blog/what-is-75hard), 75 Hard is explicitly not a fitness program — it is a "mental toughness program" designed to build discipline, self-confidence, and resilience. The physical results are a side effect of the mental work.

### Step 5: Create a Workout Log Database

Below your widgets, add a Notion database (table view) with columns: Date, Workout 1 (type + duration), Workout 2 – Outdoor (type + duration), Notes. Filter it by the current week to keep the view clean. This gives you a historical record you can look back on after 75 days.

### Step 6: Add a Diet Notes Section

Create a simple toggle block labelled "Today's Diet Log". Inside, add a text block where you note what you ate and confirm you stayed on protocol. This doesn't need to be a full food diary — just enough to hold yourself accountable.

### Step 7: Set Up a Progress Photo Reminder

In the Notion page, add a checkbox labelled "Progress photo taken". Alternatively, use the habit tracker for this. If you use Notion's reminder feature (type `@remind`), you can get a daily ping to your inbox or mobile app as a prompt.

## Tips for Staying Consistent Through 75 Days

- **Review your dashboard every morning.** Open your Notion 75 Hard page before anything else. Seeing your streak counter and water goal first thing creates an anchor for the day.
- **Log in real time, not at the end of the day.** Tap the water widget after each glass, not at 10pm when you're trying to remember if you hit 1 gallon.
- **Schedule your outdoor workout early.** Weather and schedule surprises kill outdoor workouts. Get it done by noon.
- **Keep your diet protocol simple and written down.** The more specific your rules, the less ambiguity there is when you're tired and hungry. Log it in your Notion diet notes block.
- **Use the streak counter as motivation, not pressure.** A 30-day streak is motivating. Thinking "I can't lose my 30-day streak" can also lead to rationalising shortcuts — stay honest with yourself.

## FAQs

### Do I really have to restart if I miss one task?

Yes — according to the official 75 Hard rules, missing any single daily task on any of the 75 days means restarting from day 1. There are no partial credit days and no modifications. This is by design: the restart rule is what makes the program a mental toughness test rather than a general wellness routine.

### Do I need a special Notion template for 75 Hard?

You don't need a template — the setup described above builds everything from scratch in under 10 minutes. The advantage of building your own is that it matches your exact diet protocol, workout preferences, and daily routine rather than forcing you into someone else's structure.
