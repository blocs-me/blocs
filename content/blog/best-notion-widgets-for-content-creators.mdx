## How do I add a widget to Notion?

To add a Notion widget, copy the widget's embed URL from Blocs, paste it into a Notion page, and select "Create embed" when Notion prompts you. The widget then renders live on the page, no plugin installation or code required.

### Step by step

- Open the widget page (e.g. [blocs.me/habit-tracker-widget](https://blocs.me/habit-tracker-widget)) and copy the embed URL.
- In Notion, type `/embed` and paste the URL.
- Resize the embed block to fit your content calendar or dashboard layout.
- Optional: sign in at [blocs.me/sign-in](https://blocs.me/sign-in) to save custom settings and sync across devices with Pro.
