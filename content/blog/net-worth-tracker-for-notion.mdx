## What is a net worth tracker for Notion?

A net worth tracker for Notion is a database or widget that calculates your total assets minus total liabilities and displays the result, usually alongside a trend or goal visual. In Notion specifically, this is typically built as a database with rollup or formula properties, since Notion has no built-in net worth feature.

Most setups fall into three categories: a manual database with formulas, a duplicated community template, or a database combined with an embedded visual widget for the goal-tracking piece. The database method works fine for calculation, but Notion's native progress bars are limited to percentage-of-total within a single property, which makes it awkward to show "progress toward $100K" as a clean visual without extra formula gymnastics.

## How do you track net worth in Notion?

Create a database with an Assets property, a Liabilities property, and a formula property that subtracts Liabilities from Assets. Add a Date property for monthly snapshots so you can chart the trend over time using Notion's built-in chart view.

### Step 1: Build the database

Set up columns for individual asset accounts (checking, savings, investments) and liability accounts (credit cards, loans). Use number properties formatted as currency.

### Step 2: Add the net worth formula

Create a formula property: `prop("Total Assets") - prop("Total Liabilities")`. If you're tracking individual accounts in rows rather than columns, use a rollup to sum assets and a separate rollup to sum liabilities, then subtract.

### Step 3: Snapshot monthly

Add a new row (or update values) each month with the date. Switch to a chart view grouped by date to see your net worth trend line over time, similar to the approach described in our guide on [how to track progress in Notion](https://blocs.me/blog/how-to-track-progress-in-notion).

## FAQs

### Does Notion have a built-in net worth tracker?

No. Notion has no native financial tracking feature. You have to build a database manually using number and formula properties, or duplicate a community template that already includes this setup.

### Can I automatically sync bank balances into my Notion net worth tracker?

Not natively. Notion doesn't support direct bank API connections, so most people update asset and liability numbers manually each month, or use a third-party integration tool like Zapier or Make to push data from a spreadsheet.

### What's the simplest net worth formula in Notion?

Subtract total liabilities from total assets: `prop("Assets") - prop("Liabilities")`. Add individual account rows and a rollup if you want a category-level breakdown instead of two flat numbers.

### Is there a free net worth tracker widget for Notion?

### How often should I update my net worth tracker?

Monthly is the common cadence, since most account balances and statements update on that cycle. Weekly updates are usually unnecessary noise for a net worth figure, unlike daily habit or water tracking.
