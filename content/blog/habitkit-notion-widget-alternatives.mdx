## What is HabitKit, and why look for a Notion alternative?

## How do I add a habit tracker widget to Notion?

Copy a widget's embed URL (for example, Blocs' habit tracker link), paste it on a Notion page, and select "Embed" from the menu that appears. Resize the block to fit your layout and the tracker loads live inside the page, no code or integration required.

1. Open your chosen widget's page (e.g., [blocs.me/habit-tracker-widget](https://blocs.me/habit-tracker-widget)) and copy the embed link.
2. In Notion, type `/embed` and paste the URL.
3. Resize the block and customize settings (habit name, color, goal) directly inside the widget.
