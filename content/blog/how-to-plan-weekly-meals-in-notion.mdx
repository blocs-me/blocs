## How to Set Up a Weekly Meal Planner in Notion?

Create a new full-page database, switch to Calendar view, and set the date property to "Week start." Add a "Meal type" select property with options for Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, and Snack. Each entry becomes one meal: give it a name, assign a day, and choose the meal type. You can see your entire week laid out in the calendar in under 10 minutes.

### Step 1: Create the database

Open a new Notion page, type `/database`, and select "Database - Full page." Name it "Meal Planner." This becomes the backbone of your system.

### Step 2: Add your properties

By default you get a "Name" property. Add these additional properties:

- **Date** (Date type): The day this meal is planned for.
- **Meal Type** (Select type): Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snack.
- **Status** (Select type): Planned, Cooked, Skipped.
- **Recipe Link** (URL type): Optional link to a recipe page or external URL.
- **Notes** (Text type): Ingredients to buy, substitutions, or prep notes.

### Step 3: Switch to Calendar view

Click "Add a view" and select Calendar. Set "Show by" to your Date property. Now each entry appears on the correct day of the week. You can see Monday through Sunday at a glance and drag entries between days if plans change.

### Step 4: Add a Table view for shopping

Add a second view (Table) to the same database. Filter it to show only the current week. This gives you a flat list of every planned meal, making it easy to scan for ingredients and build a shopping list. You can sort by Meal Type to group all dinners together.

### Step 5: Create a linked shopping list

On a separate page (or the same one), create a linked database view filtered to the current week. Below it, add a simple bulleted list for your shopping items. Some people prefer a separate database for recipes with a relation property linking meals to recipes. Start simple: you can always add complexity later.

## What Are the Best Notion Database Views for Meal Planning?

Calendar view works best for visualizing a full week of meals at a glance. Table view is better for editing multiple entries quickly and building shopping lists. Gallery view works well if you store recipe photos as page covers. Most meal planners use Calendar as the primary view and Table as a secondary edit view.

| View | Best For | Drawback |
|------|----------|----------|
| Calendar | Seeing the full week at a glance | Limited space to show properties |
| Table | Bulk editing, building shopping lists | No visual week layout |
| Gallery | Visual recipe browsing with cover photos | Harder to plan by day |
| Board | Grouping by Meal Type (Breakfast / Lunch / Dinner) | Doesn't show dates visually |

## How to Make Meal Planning a Consistent Weekly Habit?

Schedule a recurring 15-minute "meal planning" block in your Notion weekly review. Duplicate last week's meals as a starting template, update anything you want to change, then generate your shopping list from the Table view. Consistency comes from lowering the friction, and a Notion-based system beats a separate app because you're already in your workspace.

A few practices that help people stick to Notion meal planning:

- **Template button**: Create a Notion template button that pre-fills 7 days of empty meal slots. One click populates the week.
- **Recurring structure**: Keep certain meals fixed (e.g., pasta on Mondays, fish on Fridays) to reduce decision fatigue.

## FAQs

### Can I use Notion as a meal planner for free?

Yes. Notion's free plan includes all database features, including Calendar and Table views, which are everything you need for a functional weekly meal planner. There are no paid features required for the core meal planning setup described in this guide.

### Is there a Notion meal planning template?

Notion's template gallery includes several community-made meal planning templates. You can also build your own in about 10 minutes using the steps above. A custom build lets you match exactly how you think about food and cooking, rather than adapting to someone else's structure.

### How do I add a shopping list to my Notion meal planner?

Add a Table view of your meal database filtered to the current week. Below it, add a simple bulleted list or a separate toggle block labeled "Shopping List." Some people use a relation to a Recipes database and pull ingredients from there. The simplest approach: after planning your meals, scan the Table view and type out ingredients in a checklist on the same page.

### What widgets work well with a Notion meal planner?

### Does Notion have a built-in calendar for meal planning?

Notion has a Calendar view for databases, not a standalone calendar app. You apply the Calendar view to your meal planning database and it shows entries by date. This is sufficient for weekly meal planning. For a richer visual calendar embedded in your page, Blocs offers a [calendar widget](https://blocs.me/calendar-widget) as a Pro feature.

### Can I track nutrition goals in Notion?

You can add number properties to your meal database for calories, protein, carbs, or fat, then use rollup properties to sum totals by day or week. For habit-based nutrition tracking (e.g., "ate vegetables at every meal," "stayed under 2000 calories"), a habit tracker widget gives you a quicker daily check-in than editing a database manually.
