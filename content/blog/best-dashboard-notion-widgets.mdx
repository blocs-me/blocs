## How Do You Embed a Blocs Widget in Notion?

Embedding a Blocs widget in Notion takes about 60 seconds. Notion's native embed block accepts any iframe URL, so no extensions or integrations are needed.

1. Go to the widget page on [blocs.me](https://blocs.me) and copy the embed URL (e.g., `https://blocs.me/pomodoro`)
2. In Notion, type `/embed` and press Enter
3. Paste the URL into the embed dialog and click "Embed link"
4. Resize the embed block by dragging the corners to fit your dashboard layout

For a polished dashboard, use Notion's column layout to place multiple widgets side by side — for example, a clock and a progress bar in a two-column row at the top, then a habit tracker and water tracker below.
