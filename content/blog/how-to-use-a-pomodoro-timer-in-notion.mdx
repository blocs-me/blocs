## Why use a Pomodoro method instead of just setting a phone timer?

Structured 25/5 intervals reduce the mental friction of deciding when to take breaks, which lowers burnout risk during long work blocks compared to open-ended "work until tired" sessions. Research on [scheduled breaks and sustained attention from the American Psychological Association](https://www.apa.org/topics/healthy-workplaces/break-productivity-mental-health) supports taking regular short breaks to maintain focus over multi-hour work sessions. Keeping the timer inside Notion (rather than a phone or separate app) also means you're not tempted to check notifications when you glance at the time.
