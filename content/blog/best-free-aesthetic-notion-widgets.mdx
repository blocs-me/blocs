## What Are Aesthetic Notion Widgets?

Aesthetic Notion widgets are embeddable mini-apps you add to a Notion page using an iframe embed block. They display live, interactive content — timers, trackers, clocks, weather — styled to match a clean, minimal workspace rather than clashing with your layout. The best ones look like they belong inside Notion natively, not bolted on from the outside.

Most Notion users discover widgets when they want to upgrade a dashboard from plain text and databases into something visually engaging. A ticking Pomodoro timer, a colored habit grid, or a live weather display adds ambient context without requiring you to open another app or tab.

## How Do You Embed Free Aesthetic Widgets in Notion?

Embedding a Blocs widget in Notion takes under a minute. Open any Notion page, type `/embed`, paste the widget URL (e.g., `https://blocs.me/pomodoro`), and press Enter. The widget renders live inside your page — no extensions, no integrations, no code required.

### Step-by-step: Adding a Widget to Notion

1. Open the Notion page where you want the widget
2. Click an empty block and type `/embed`, then select "Embed"
3. Paste the widget URL from blocs.me (e.g., `https://blocs.me/water-tracker`)
4. Press Enter or click "Embed link"
5. Resize the embed block by dragging the edges to fit your layout

For the free widgets, no account is required. Your data persists in your browser locally. Pro users get cloud sync so their streak and tracker data follows them across devices.

### Are Blocs widgets really free?

Yes. The Pomodoro Timer, Habit Tracker, and Water Tracker are permanently free with no account required. You embed them via Notion's `/embed` block using the widget URLs at blocs.me. There's no trial period — they're free indefinitely with default settings.

### Do embedded widgets slow down Notion?

Minimal impact. Blocs widgets are lightweight iframes that load independently of the rest of your Notion page. Notion itself handles the iframe rendering; the widgets don't affect your page's database queries or other blocks.

### Do Notion widgets work on mobile?

Notion's mobile app has limited iframe embed support. Widgets generally display correctly on the Notion web app and desktop app. On mobile, embeds may show a "view source" link rather than rendering inline depending on your Notion app version and settings.
