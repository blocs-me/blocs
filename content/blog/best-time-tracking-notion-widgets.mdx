## What's the difference between a Pomodoro timer and a countdown timer?

A Pomodoro timer repeats short work/break intervals for ongoing focus sessions, while a countdown timer tracks time remaining until a single fixed date or deadline. Use Pomodoro for daily deep work and Countdown for launches, exams, or project due dates.
